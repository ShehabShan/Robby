# P-006 — Plan summary for owner approval (S7, 2026-10-30)

Plain-language summary of the full plan (GDD v2, ART, ARCHITECTURE, ROADMAP). Building starts only after the owner approves.

## The game in one line
A cunning-cultivation action RPG on Roblox: catch and bond living creatures (**Kith**) that give you your powers,
grow your inner **Core** through Ranks, and outwit rivals across five regions (**Reaches**). Inspired by Reverend
Insanity, but every name, place and story is our own.

## What makes it good
1. **Your build is your collection.** Every move comes from a Kith you found, bonded (a tug-of-war against its will),
   fed and fused. Your first Kith becomes your **Heart Kith** — your identity, never lost.
2. **Combat that feels great.** Rell Seas-level hits: instant response, hit-stop, effects and sound on every hit.
3. **Smart beats grind.** Combine Kith into big combo moves (**Weaves**) at the Weave Loom; Paths that help or fight
   each other; name your own **Signature Weave**. No "click to raise a stat" loops.
4. **A world with politics.** Each region has its own society. Join the **Clans** (safety, stipends) or the
   **Rogues** (freedom, bounties, black market). Neither is "the good side".
5. **Fair money.** Only cosmetics and convenience are sold. Kith, power and Ranks are never for sale.

## How we build it
- **M1 vertical slice:** the starting valley (Mistvale), 1 hunting zone, a bonding grove, 1 boss, 4 Kith, Ranks 1→2,
  the first 10 minutes polished. Then playtest with you.
- **M2–M5:** more content (server-wide beast surges, dungeons), progression and economy, polish, soft launch.
- Built and tested headlessly with the Roblox Open Cloud key (already proven to work). Nothing is published without your OK.
