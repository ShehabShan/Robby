Top-left: Core orb (colour = Rank hue), red health, green Qi, Stage pips.   Right thumb: big Attack, Dash, Guard, and one button per equipped Kith.   On each Kith button: orange arc = Strain (full = Kith rests ~20 s); small dot = Satiety (green fed, amber "!" hungry); dark sweep = cooldown.   Purple diamond: Weave, lights up when Qi is enough.   Enemy tag: amber ⚠ = one Rank above you.

Design goal: Strain and Satiety live on the Kith buttons, not as extra bars, so the phone screen keeps only two bars (health, Qi).
