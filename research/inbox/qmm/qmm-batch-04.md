## 2. Places and layout
*   **Alliance Slope:** Originally an ordinary mountain slope where the 2nd generation clan head created the first alliance. Expanded ten times over the years into a giant town square along the mountain wall. Features a huge erected rock with carved buildings and rock furniture where the clan heads sit to watch events below (ch 124, 125, 126).
*   **Stone crack secret cave:** Descending crude rock stairs lead to an artificial stone door carved with the words: "Golden Centipede's cave is precarious, Earth Communication is the way to avoid the disaster." Behind the door is a dark, straight, long tunnel that houses a centipede colony (ch 128, 129).
*   **Village Square:** Located in front of the family head pavilion. During the wolf tide, it features a stage for exchanging merit points and a large banner utilizing Swimword Gu to display the battle merit board and resource board (ch 127, 131).
*   **Gu Yue Village outer walls and defenses:** Heavily reinforced for the wolf tide. Features trenches outside filled with sharp bamboo poles. The outer walls are planted with defensive Steel Vine Gu and Poison Flower Gu. Dozens of thick stone defensive towers, which are built higher than the walls, serve as the main attack platforms. Behind the walls, residential buildings and taverns are remodeled into temporary forts for support and healing teams. The eastern gate is destroyed by a disguised frenzy lightning wolf (ch 149, 150).
*   **Underground cave / Primeval spring:** Located in the basement of the village, highly hidden. Discovered by the 1st generation clan head. It houses the primeval spring that produces primeval stones and an underground river. Contains the clan's secret room (ch 155, 156).
*   **Secret room:** Located inside the underground cave. Secured by a rock door that turns virtual when a specific Gu knocks on it. The room is 666 ⅔ square meters. Illuminated by Water Light Gu embedded in the walls. Contains tens of rock tables, each half a human's height, holding a white jade plate with a Digital Shade Gu. Used to store the clan's refinement recipes (ch 155, 156).
*   **Dark mountain cave:** A remote, unfamiliar cave outside the village. Dim and eerie. Contains a large rock where Fang Yuan ties Gu Yue Yao Le. Used as the site for refining the Man-beast Life Burial Gu (ch 150, 151).
*   **Graveyard:** Located on a slope behind the village. Fills rapidly during the wolf tide (ch 143).
*   **Chi family manor:** Includes Gu Yue Chi Lian's study room where he conducts private meetings and signs loan receipts with Fang Yuan, and a secret room for closed-door cultivation (ch 152, 154).

## 3. Factions and power
*   **Gu Yue Clan:** Forms a three-clan alliance for the wolf tide, setting up a battle merit board where one lightning wolf eyeball equals 10 points (ch 127). The medicine faction is initially led by Gu Yue Yao Ji, who tries to monopolize vitality leaves, but she loses power after her granddaughter's death and Fang Yuan's political attacks (ch 145, 153). Gu Yue Chi Zhong takes over temporarily to balance the Chi and Yao factions (ch 153, 157). Rank 3 Gu Masters automatically become clan elders (ch 152). Elders receive 100 primeval stones a week (300 during the wolf tide), a free Rank 3 Gu, access to the secret room's recipes, and the right to marry one wife and two concubines (ch 154, 155). 
*   **Bai Clan:** A newly rising clan that lives by a waterfall behind the mountain. Their Gu Masters have whitish skin and a Yin aura (ch 124). They produce the Fish Scale Gu (ch 123). They possess Qing Mao Mountain's top genius, Bai Ning Bing, who has shifted the power balance (ch 121, 125). 
*   **Xiong Clan:** Situated at the mountain foot, making them the first to suffer wolf pack attacks. Their Gu Masters are robust and display physical strength (ch 124). They get surrounded by wolves three times and require reinforcements from the other clans (ch 147).

## 4. People
*   **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** C grade aptitude. Advances to Rank 3 and becomes a clan elder by kidnapping Yao Le and feeding her to a bear to refine the Man-beast Life Burial Gu (ch 150, 151). Blackmails Gu Yue Chi Lian to act as his political shield and extorts him for primeval stones and Gu (ch 153, 154). Displays a purely demonic, detached nature (ch 151). 
*   **Bai Ning Bing:** Bai clan's symbol of rise, Rank 3. Possesses the Northern Dark Ice Soul physique (one of the Ten Extreme physiques), giving him 100% primeval essence but guaranteeing he will explode unless he reaches Rank 6 (ch 135). Dilutes his essence to Rank 2 to delay death and fights for fun (ch 135). Massacres several Gu Yue Gu Masters and self-detonates his right arm (ch 134, 139).
*   **Gu Yue Qing Shu:** Rank 2 peak stage. A humble, kind, and devoted Gu Master who serves as a role model. Sacrifices himself by overusing the Wood Charm Gu, turning into a dead tree spirit to trap Bai Ning Bing and protect the clan (ch 141, 142).
*   **Gu Yue Yao Ji:** Medicine hall elder. A shrewd, veteran politician who tries to steal Fang Yuan's Nine Leaf Vitality Grass (ch 145). She vomits blood and faints when Fang Yuan and Chi Lian publicly attack her dereliction of duty after her granddaughter goes missing (ch 153).
*   **Gu Yue Yao Le:** Yao Ji's granddaughter, Rank 1 healer. Cute, kind, and naive. Complains about Fang Yuan to her grandmother (ch 147). Saved from wolves by Fang Yuan, only to be kidnapped, stripped, and eaten alive by a bear to refine a Gu (ch 150).
*   **Gu Yue Chi Lian:** Chi faction head. Blackmailed by Fang Yuan because he faked his grandson Chi Cheng's aptitude (who is actually C grade). Forced to protect Fang Yuan politically and "lend" him primeval stones and the Cleansing Water Gu (ch 153, 154).
*   **Gu Yue Chi Zhong:** Rank 3 middle stage. Temporarily replaces Yao Ji as medicine hall elder to balance factions. Trades a Prize Token to Fang Yuan for the Nine Leaf Vitality Grass to establish his authority (ch 157).
*   **Xiong Li:** Strongest Rank 2 in QMM. Uses Brown Bear Innate Strength Gu and Grand Bear Gu for the strength of two bears. Killed by Bai Ning Bing (ch 123, 137).
*   **Xiong Jiao Man:** Xiong clan Gu Master who rides and controls giant bears. Extorted by Fang Yuan for her Bear Enslavement Gu and 700 primeval stones when trapped by wolves (ch 146, 147).
*   **Gu Yue Ye:** Retired Rank 3 elder whose cultivation dropped to Rank 2 upper stage. Forced to fight in the wolf tide. Humiliated by Fang Yuan when buying vitality leaves (ch 145, 146).

## 5. Gu and cultivation
*   **Cultivation levels:** Rank 1 (green copper), Rank 2 (red steel: initial/light red, middle/scarlet, upper/crimson, peak/dark red), Rank 3 (white silver) (ch 122, 145, 151). Ten Extreme physiques (like Northern Dark Ice Soul) have 100% primeval essence, allowing insane cultivation speed but causing the aperture to eventually explode from pressure (ch 135, 140).
*   **Four Flavours Liquor worm:** Rank 2. Refines upper stage primeval essence. Fused from 2 Liquor worms and four wines (redberry, golden honey, bitter shell, white grains liquid) using 400 primeval stones. Flashes with neon-like colors (ch 121).
*   **Stealth Scales Gu:** Rank 2. Conceals the body and clothes. Fused using Stealth Rock Gu and Fish Scale Gu. Looks like a fossilized carp (ch 123, 127).
*   **Earth Communication Ear Grass:** Rank 2. Scouts up to 300 paces when roots are pierced into the ground. Eats ginseng roots. Must be grafted onto a severed ear (ch 128, 129).
*   **Chainsaw Golden Centipede:** Rank 3 wild Gu. 1 meter long, 2 fists wide, with sharp saw-like teeth on its sides that buzz like a chainsaw (ch 129).
*   **Plunder Gu:** Rank 2. Steals a Gu from an enemy, but requires continuous primeval essence and holding still (ch 131, 138).
*   **Water Shield Gu:** Defensive Gu that forms a spherical barrier of condensed moisture. Weakness: loses effectiveness if the user is against a wall and cannot form a ball. Looted by Fang Yuan (ch 134, 136, 143).
*   **Stone Aperture Gu:** Rank 3. Turns the aperture wall into stone, instantly forcing peak stage cultivation but halting all future progress and removing natural primeval essence regeneration (ch 143, 144).
*   **Man-beast Life Burial Gu:** Rank 3. A demonic breakthrough Gu to go from Rank 2 to 3. Requires a virgin girl with Gu Master aptitude to be eaten entirely alive by a beast controlled by an Enslavement Gu, then refined with fire and poison. Looks like a black spider with a bear head and a blood tattoo of the girl's face. Leaves a black-red impurity in the user's primeval essence (ch 145, 151).
*   **Sky Canopy Gu:** Rank 3. A large milky-white ladybug with dark spots. Fused from White Jade Gu and an aqua defense Gu (ch 154).
*   **Thunderwings Gu:** Rank 3. Grants temporary flight via materialized thunder wings (ch 154).
*   **Cleansing Water Gu:** Rank 3. Washes away impurities in the aperture. Extorted by Fang Yuan from Chi Lian (ch 151, 154).
*   **Wood Charm Gu:** Rank 3. Turns the user into a wood charm tree spirit, allowing them to absorb natural essence from the air. Overuse corrodes the body into dead wood (ch 126, 141).
*   **Frost Demon Gu:** Rank 3. Transforms the user into a frost demon. Corrodes the body to ice if overused (ch 137).
*   **Ice Muscle Gu:** Rank 3. Turns muscles to transparent blue ice for ultimate defense (ch 137).
*   **Blue Bird Ice Coffin Gu:** Rank 3. Flies at a target and explodes, freezing them in a block of ice (ch 136, 141).
*   **Shadow Bond Gu:** Rank 2. Links the user's shadow to an enemy's; the enemy takes 10% of any damage the user suffers (ch 125, 137).
*   **Digital Shade Gu:** Records refinement recipes. Color-coded: green (Rank 1), red-black (Rank 2), white (Rank 3), yellow (Rank 4), purple (Rank 5) (ch 155).
*   **Blood Moon Gu:** Rank 3. Fused from Moonglow Gu and Blood Essence Gu. Fires a blood-red moonblade that causes continuous bleeding. Easy to feed (eats fresh blood), but loses 2/3 of its power for a few days a month when it bleeds uncontrollably (ch 157).

## 6. Creatures and nature
*   **Lightning wolves:** Have sharp, eagle-like vision but poor smell, making them vulnerable to Stealth Scales Gu (ch 127).
*   **Crippled wolves:** Sickly or injured wolves expelled from the nest in winter. Cunning and travel in small packs (ch 122).
*   **Bold lightning wolf:** 100-beast king. The size of a calf with silver fangs. Some possess Gu worms that grant lightning armor or leaping ability (ch 130, 134).
*   **Frenzy lightning wolf:** 1000-beast king. The size of an elephant. Highly dangerous, usually hosting several Rank 2 and a Rank 3 wild Gu (e.g., Thunderclap Gu, Thunder Wings Gu, Thunder Roar Gu, Aura Restraint Gu, Soundwave Gu) (ch 131, 149).
*   **Bears:** Giant brown and black bears, controlled by Gu Masters using the Bear Enslavement Gu for combat or refinement (ch 146, 150).

## 7. Events and conflicts
*   **Three Clan Gu Battle Tournament:** Held at Alliance Slope to spar and build trust for the wolf tide. Xiong Jiang defeats Bai Bing Yi using Shadow Bond Gu. Xiong Li challenges Fang Yuan, who instantly admits defeat. Qing Shu defeats Xiong Li using Wood Charm Gu (ch 125, 126).
*   **Wolf Tide Outbreak:** Begins with crippled wolves in winter, escalating to healthy wolves and bold lightning wolves in summer. A massive wave of thousands of wolves, led by frenzy lightning wolves, directly assaults Gu Yue village, breaking the eastern gate (ch 130, 149).
*   **Bai Ning Bing's Rampage:** Suppresses his cultivation to Rank 2 to fight for fun. He slaughters Gu Yue Man Shi's group, kills Xiong Zhan, and wipes out Xiong Li's group (killing Xiong Li) (ch 131, 134, 137).
*   **Qing Shu vs. Bai Ning Bing:** An intense, destructive battle on a mountain path. Qing Shu fully activates the Wood Charm Gu, turning into a giant tree spirit. Bai Ning Bing is forced to self-detonate his arm to survive. Qing Shu sacrifices his life, turning into dead wood to trap Bai Ning Bing (ch 139, 140, 141, 142).
*   **Yao Le's Murder and Refinement:** Fang Yuan saves Yao Le from the wolf attack, knocks her out, and takes her to a dark cave. He ties her up, strips her, and forces an enslaved black bear to eat her alive (including her bones and brain). He then burns the bear alive from the inside to refine the Man-beast Life Burial Gu, stepping into Rank 3 (ch 149, 150, 151).
*   **Elder Meeting and Yao Ji's Downfall:** Yao Ji demands to inspect Fang Yuan's aperture after his sudden Rank 3 advancement. Fang Yuan refuses, claiming Chi Lian backed his closed-door cultivation. He then attacks Yao Ji for wasting medicine hall resources to search for her granddaughter. Chi Lian is forced to agree. Yao Ji vomits blood and faints, losing her position (ch 152, 153).

## 8. Mood and time
*   The batch progresses from winter to summer. Winter is bleak, snowy, and freezing, with starving crippled wolves roaming. Spring brings blooming flowers but deceptive peace as wolves mate. Summer is scorching hot, filled with massive thunderstorms and the iron smell of blood as the true wolf tide hits.
*   The tone becomes incredibly ruthless, tragic, and pragmatic. Clansmen die in droves, old retired elders are forced to sacrifice themselves, and funerals are common. Fang Yuan's detachment reaches a peak, highlighting his pure demonic nature as he tortures a girl to death for power without a shred of empathy or malice—viewing all life as equal and insignificant (ch 143, 145, 151).

## 9. Recurring objects
*   **Battle merit board / Resource board:** Giant banners in the village square utilizing Swimword Gu to display rankings and exchangeable goods (ch 127, 131).
*   **Prize Token / Reward Contributions:** A triangular token with smooth corners that grants access to the underground Gu cave to pick rare Gu worms (ch 157).
*   **Receipt for loan:** Documents Fang Yuan signs with a fingerprint when he extorts "loans" of primeval stones and Gu from Chi Lian (ch 154).
