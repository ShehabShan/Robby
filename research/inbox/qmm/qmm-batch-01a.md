## 2. Places and layout:
* **Qing Mao Mountain:** Vast, uneven mountain terrain experiencing spring weather (rain and breezes) (ch 1, 3, 7, 22). Covered in green spear bamboo forests, wild flowers, and flowing streams (ch 7, 13). Houses the Gu Yue, Xiong, and Bai villages (ch 1, 22). At night, it features dozens of tiny lights forming a bright band (ch 1). 
* **Gu Yue Village:** Situated on the mountain side down to the foot (ch 1). Contains a few thousand tall buildings made of green bamboo and wood, built on massive wooden stakes due to the uneven terrain (ch 1, 3, 10). Contains three taverns (ch 12) and one inn (ch 11). Has a huge entrance gate leading to the outskirts (ch 9). Wide streets (ch 9). Used by Gu Yue clansmen and mortals.
* **Gu Yue clan pavilion:** Located in the middle of the village (ch 1). A magnificent five-storey pavilion with sharp tilted roofs, heavily guarded (ch 4). The third-floor garret has windows overlooking half the village (ch 1). Contains a grand hall with a tall black three-layered case housing ancestor memorial tablets (ch 1). Used by clan elders and the clan head for ceremonies and meetings (ch 1, 4).
* **Underground cave:** Reached via a constructed stone ladder going down from the clan pavilion (ch 4). Features rainbow-sparkling stalactites (ch 4). Deep inside is a 3 zhang wide underground river emitting faint blue light, with clear water and a sandy bottom (ch 4). Opposite the river bank is a flower sea of moon orchids (blue and pink crescent petals, jade stems, pearl-like centers) that emit primeval qi pressure (ch 4, 5). Houses the spirit spring, the foundation of the village (ch 4). Used by the academy for the Awakening Ceremony (ch 4-6).
* **Uncle's House (Fang Yuan & Fang Zheng's home):** A tall bamboo/wooden stilt house where the second floor is residential (ch 3). Has a dim living room (ch 9) and a pond with aquatic plants and fish outside (ch 10). Fang Zheng gets moved to a room twice as large, featuring a rosewood desk and a soft carpet (ch 9). Used by Uncle, Aunt, the twins, and their servants (ch 3, 9).
* **Village Academy:** Contains a classroom with at least 57 desks for new students. Has windows overlooking distant verdant mountains and green spear bamboo groves (ch 7). Has dormitories for qualified students (ch 16).
* **Gu room:** Located beside the academy, 60 square meters in size (ch 8). Four walls are embedded with varying sizes of square holes holding containers (grey stone basins, jade dishes, grass cages) with noisy Rank 1 Gu (ch 8). One wall has a row of silver plates holding Moonlight Gu (ch 8). Heavily guarded outside (ch 8).
* **Village inn:** The only inn in the village, mostly deserted outside of merchant caravan season (ch 11). Three floors. The first floor is a cafeteria with damp large marble tiles, thick wooden pillars, and square tables with long benches (ch 11). Sells premium green bamboo wine (ch 12). Second and third floors contain tidy guest rooms (ch 11). Used by visiting traders, hunters, and occasionally Gu Masters (ch 11, 12, 17).
* **Bamboo forest outskirts:** Surrounds the village, filled with green spear bamboo straight like lines, grassy ground, white and yellow wild flowers, mossy stones, and pebbles (ch 7, 13, 14). Crossed by a quiet mountain stream (ch 14).
* **Waterfall and Benchland:** Located behind Qing Mao Mountain (ch 14). A huge waterfall pounds out a deep pool next to the Bai Clan Village, branching into smaller falls (ch 14). A shallow benchland filled with various sized pebbles sits at the base of one of the branches (ch 14).
* **Secret cave (Flower Wine Monk's tomb):** Hidden behind a branch waterfall at the benchland (ch 14). Accessed through a large, water-battered crevice in a huge gray boulder leaning against the mountain wall (ch 14). The passage is tight, dark, and mossy (ch 14). Opens into an 80 square meter enclosed space inside the mountain bathed in dim red light (ch 14). The air is dry, and the walls are covered in withered vines (Wine Sack Flower Gu and Rice Pouch Grass Gu) (ch 14). Contains a pile of bones, primeval stones, and a Photo-audio Gu projecting on the wall (ch 14, 15, 16).
* **Bai Clan Village:** Situated next to the deep pool pounded out by the huge waterfall behind the mountain (ch 14).
* **Academy Training Field:** 3 Mu in size, with flat ground paved in thick grey graphite (ch 22). Enclosed by a tight circular wall of green spear bamboo, with wild roses growing at the corners (ch 22). Used for target practice with Scarecrow Gu puppets (ch 22).

## 3. Factions and power:
* **Gu Yue Clan:** Settled on the mountain hundreds of years ago after finding the spirit spring (ch 4).
  * **Leaders/Elders:** Gu Yue Bo (Clan head, middle-aged, high rank, ch 1). Academy elder (Rank 3, runs the academy, ch 7, 20, 22). Gu Yue Mo Chen (Elder of Mo branch, ch 4, 21). Gu Yue Chi Lian (Elder of Chi branch, ch 4, 21). Gu Yue Dong Tu (Fang Yuan's Uncle, Rank 2, ch 9, 18).
  * **Strength/Resources:** Mostly relies on the spirit spring and moon orchids (ch 4, 15). The Moonlight Gu is their clan symbol (ch 8, 22). Historically had two Rank 5 masters (ch 7).
  * **Internal runnings:** Operates a yearly Awakening Ceremony (ch 1, 4). Academy awards primeval stones for passing tests (ch 7, 22). By clan rules, an eldest son inherits family property at 16 only if he reaches Rank 1 middle stage (ch 9, 18). Uses mortal servants and slaves (ch 3, 9).
* **Bai Clan:** Neighboring clan located by the waterfall/pool (ch 14). Has strong influence comparable to Gu Yue (ch 14). Features super genius Bai Ning Bing, who reached Rank 3 in two years (ch 1). Their signature Gu is the Stream Gu (ch 22).
* **Xiong Clan:** Another clan on the mountain. Their signature Gu is the Bear Strength Gu (ch 1, 22).
* **Relations:** The three clans constantly compete over resources and talented youths (ch 1).

## 4. People:
* **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** Protagonist, demonic path Gu Master. Reborn with 500 years of experience, C-grade talent (ch 1, 2, 6). Vital Gu is the Spring Autumn Cicada, later refines Liquor worm and Moonlight Gu (ch 19, 20). Ruthless, perceptive, and cold, he easily ignores mortal attachments to secure his cultivation path. Often found drinking at the inn or wandering the outskirts. Wins the Gu refinement test in this batch (ch 20).
* **Gu Yue Fang Zheng:** Fang Yuan's twin brother, A-grade talent (ch 2, 6). Initially submissive and insecure, he quickly becomes arrogant and resentful of his brother after being favored by the clan (ch 2, 8, 18). Vital Gu is the Moonlight Gu (ch 21). Lives with Uncle and develops a crush on Shen Cui (ch 9, 18).
* **Uncle (Gu Yue Dong Tu):** Rank 2 Gu Master. Greedy and scheming (ch 9, 18). Secretly plots with his wife to delay Fang Yuan's cultivation so they can permanently steal his parents' inheritance (ch 18).
* **Aunt:** Uncle's wife. Hysterical and greedy, aids in the plot to frame Fang Yuan and steal the inheritance (ch 9, 18).
* **Shen Cui:** Fang Yuan's personal servant girl, daughter of Mother Shen (ch 3). Vain and scheming, she tries to seduce Fang Yuan to frame him but is terrified by his murderous intent (ch 3, 11). Later reassigned to Fang Zheng [implied] (ch 18).
* **Mother Shen:** Housekeeper of Uncle's house, commands all domestic slaves (ch 3, 9).
* **Academy Elder:** Rank 3 Gu Master with white hair and beard (ch 4, 7). Teaches the youths, judges their progress, and hands out primeval stone rewards (ch 4, 20, 22).
* **Gu Yue Mo Bei:** B-grade talent, grandson of elder Mo Chen (ch 4, 21). Tall and sturdy with a brave aura (ch 4). Demoralized after Fang Yuan beats him in refinement (ch 21).
* **Gu Yue Chi Chen:** C-grade talent (faked as B-grade by his grandfather Chi Lian) (ch 4). Small with a pockmarked face. Motivated to keep practicing by his grandfather (ch 21).
* **Jiang Ya:** Rank 1 upper stage Gu Master, around 20 years old (ch 12). A logistics Gu Master. Arrogant toward mortals, but backs down when realizing Fang Yuan is a Gu Master. Found drinking at the inn (ch 12).
* **Flower Wine Monk:** Rank 5 demonic Gu Master from 300 years ago (ch 15). Bald, wore pink robes (ch 15). Ambushed and killed the fourth clan head, but died of his wounds, leaving behind a secret tomb and the Liquor worm (ch 14, 15).
* **Fourth generation clan head:** Rank 5 Gu Master (ch 7). Revered as a hero, but secretly treacherous. Begged for mercy before sneak-attacking the Flower Wine Monk, leading to his own death (ch 15).

## 5. Gu and cultivation:
* **Ranks & Cultivation:** Cultivation has 9 major ranks (1-9), each divided into initial, middle, upper, and peak stages (ch 7). Rank 1 uses green copper essence, Rank 2 uses red iron, Rank 3 uses white silver (ch 6, 22).
* **Aperture & Talent:** An invisible spherical space below the navel holding the primeval sea (ch 5, 6). A-grade holds 80-90% essence (can reach Rank 5), B-grade holds 60-70% (reaches Rank 3-4), C-grade holds 40-50% (reaches Rank 2), D-grade holds 20-30% (reaches Rank 1-2) (ch 6, 7).
* **Refining Gu:** A battle of will between the Gu Master's essence and the wild Gu's survival instinct. Drains massive amounts of primeval essence. Can be expedited by using a higher rank Gu's aura to suppress the wild Gu's will (ch 10, 17, 19).
* **Spring Autumn Cicada:** Rank 6. Allows rebirth by traveling upstream in the river of time (ch 2). Currently extremely weak with withered yellow wings, but emits a massive aura that easily subdues lower rank Gu (ch 19). Used by Fang Yuan as his vital Gu (ch 19).
* **Hope Gu:** Tiny lights that gather to open a mortal's aperture (ch 5).
* **Moonlight Gu:** Rank 1. The Gu Yue clan symbol, looks like a blue crystal crescent (ch 8). Shoots a pale blue moonblade attack costing 10% primeval essence (ch 22). Used by Fang Zheng and Fang Yuan (ch 20, 21).
* **Liquor worm:** Rank 1. Silkworm-like, pearl white, smells of wine, feeds on wine (ch 14, 17). Refines primeval essence to one minor stage higher (e.g., Rank 1 initial to middle stage) (ch 17). Carried the Flower Wine Monk's will until subdued by Fang Yuan (ch 19).
* **Single Gate Poison Gu:** Used by Flower Wine Monk to poison the fourth clan head (ch 15).
* **Moonshadow Gu:** Rank 4. Restricts usage of primeval sea and essence. Used by fourth clan head to doom Flower Wine Monk (ch 15).
* **Photo-audio Gu:** Records and projects sound/imagery. Feeds on light and sound (ch 16).
* **Wine Sack Flower Gu & Rice Pouch Grass Gu:** Rank 1 natural Gu. Grow wine and rice when fed primeval essence (ch 14).
* **Scarecrow Gu:** Rank 1. Grass puppet used for target practice, self-recovers from cuts (ch 22).
* **Money/Primeval stones:** Greyish white oval stones holding natural primeval essence. Used as currency and to refill apertures. Shrinks as essence is drained (ch 8, 18). 1 stone lasts a mortal family a month (ch 11). Good wine costs 2 stones (ch 12). 

## 6. Creatures and nature:
* **Predicament:** Mythical wild beasts from Ren Zu's legend that hunt humans (ch 5).
* **Dragonpill crickets:** Insects active at night that emit red light like agate and bounce like water (ch 13, 14).
* **Star fireflies:** Red bugs that burst from clouds in the bamboo forest when disturbed (ch 14).
* **Colourful peacock parrots:** Eagle-sized birds with rainbow feathers, parrot beaks, and peacock tails that fly in large groups during spring (ch 22).
* **Wild beasts:** Black skinned wild boar, pheasants, and hares hunted by mortal mountain men (ch 12).

## 7. Events and conflicts:
* **Awakening Ceremony (ch 4-6):** Youths walk across an underground river toward a flower sea to awaken their apertures. Fang Yuan gets C-grade (27 steps) while Fang Zheng gets A-grade (43 steps), drastically shifting the clan's favor and altering the brothers' dynamic.
* **Flower Wine Monk battle (ch 15):** Occurred 300 years ago. The fourth clan head begged for mercy, then sneak-attacked the Flower Wine Monk with the Moonshadow Gu. The Monk killed the clan head and elders before fleeing to die. The surviving elders rewrote history, keeping the truth hidden.
* **Gu Refinement Test (ch 10, 17, 19, 20):** Academy students race to refine their vital Gu. Fang Yuan wins the 20 primeval stone prize by using the Spring Autumn Cicada's aura to instantly subdue the Liquor worm and Moonlight Gu, shocking the entire clan.
* **Inn Conflict (ch 12):** Gu master Jiang Ya destroys a wine jar to intimidate a group of mortal hunters who flaunted their wealth, highlighting the immense power gap and financial strain of Gu Masters.

## 8. Mood and time:
* **Season and Weather:** Spring. The arc begins with a quiet spring night rain (ch 1). It rains heavily for three days and nights straight (ch 10), then clears up to beautiful moonlit nights (ch 13) and warm, vibrant spring days with blooming flowers (ch 22).
* **Time:** The events of this batch span roughly three weeks: one week from Awakening to the start of the academy (ch 7), a few days of rain, and 10 days post-refinement (ch 22).
* **Tone:** Bleak, ruthless, and pragmatic. The world operates strictly on survival of the fittest. Mortals are treated as inferior. Family ties are easily discarded for greed (Uncle/Aunt) or jealousy (Fang Zheng). Fang Yuan is cold and calculating, viewing everyone merely as stepping stones or obstacles.

## 9. Recurring objects:
* **Primeval stones:** Greyish white, oval stones used as currency and cultivation fuel. They physically shrink and get lighter as essence is drained.
* **Green bamboo wine:** The best wine in the village, highly fragrant, costs 2 primeval stones, and successfully lures the Liquor worm.
* **Moonlight Gu mark:** Manifests as a pale blue crescent mark on the user's forehead or palm when refined.
