## 2. Places and layout
*   **Merchant tentage area:** Outside Gu Yue village on a grass turf. Bustling at night. The ground inside shops is uncovered mountain rock/soil. Becomes a muddy, pothole-filled mess of garbage after the caravan leaves (ch 44, 47).
*   **Wine shop (tent):** Inside the merchant area. Decorated with live, colorful "Innocent Mushroom" Gu grown as seating (some table-sized, some bench-sized). A long counter sits at the back. Dozens of transparent crystal ladybugs cling upside-down to the blue tent walls, holding various wines (ch 44, 45).
*   **Mountain path:** Road connecting the village to the outside. Starts as cobblestone for 500 meters, then turns into a muddy, narrow, tortuous mountain trail resembling goat intestines. Shaded by dense forests of green spear bamboo, pine, and cypress (ch 47, 59).
*   **Secret cave & Flower Wine Monk's tunnel:** Inside the mountain, accessed via a narrow rock crack. The image wall (which plays a recording) can be shaved down to reveal a hidden passage (ch 46, 60). The tunnel slants diagonally downward. It is narrow at first, widens after 50 steps, and is tall/wide enough to swing arms after 100 steps. It is roughly 300 meters long, completely walled with dark red soil that emits a faint red glow. At the end, a smooth, round giant rock blocks the path on an incline (ch 61, 79).
*   **Second secret room:** Located past the giant round rock (once pushed 50 steps up a straight platform). Empty, with four red-glowing soil walls and a simple grey rock door at the far end. An Earth Treasury Flower Gu was buried in the damp soil here (ch 79).
*   **Underground stone forest:** Found behind the grey rock door. A natural underground space inside Qing Mao Mountain, over 30 Mu large and 16 meters tall. Large dark red stone pillars hang from the stone ceiling like inverted trees without branches. The pillars are full of dark holes serving as nests for jade eye stone monkeys. The space is dimly lit by red glowing walls (ch 79).
*   **Academy Gu storage room:** A small room storing Rank 1 Gu worms. Has a small skylight on the roof casting a golden rectangular light. The four walls are filled with square holes of varying sizes holding rock pots, jade pots, grass cages, and porcelain ware (ch 62).
*   **Chi family secret room:** Small room with limestone walls, lit by a single torch. Contains a praying mat. Used by Chi Lian to secretly transfer primeval essence to Chi Cheng (ch 50).
*   **Gu Yue Bo's courtyard:** A concealed, quiet courtyard. Dominated by a large locust tree with complicated branches forming a jasper-like canopy over the entire space (ch 63).
*   **Old man Wang's wooden house:** Located at the mountain foot. Surrounded by a tall green spear bamboo fence. Has vegetable fields and a man-made well. The house is built from solid thick bark, covered in green moss, and has four rooms (including a living room and kitchen). Contains a firepit with a chimney where a bamboo tube map was hidden (ch 67, 68).
*   **Hidden treehouse:** One of three safe houses marked by red circles on the Wang family map. Hidden in a huge tree in the forest. Small, messy, with a dark yellow carpet, an iron stove, a bronze kettle, and two windows connected by ropes for drying clothes (ch 73, 74).
*   **Mountain forest / hunting grounds:** Spans the mountain. Features tall woods, fallen leaves, bushes, and streams. Inhabited by deer, bears, wild boars, and wolves. Filled with hidden hunter traps (ch 65, 77, 80). Deep within sits a red cross zone on the map, home to a massive wild boar king (ch 77).
*   **Academy arena / training grounds:** The stage for combat exams. Surrounded by bamboo walls. During the year-end exam, three stages are built side-by-side, with tents, long tables, and chairs set up for observing elders (ch 48, 82).

## 3. Factions and power
*   **Gu Yue Clan:**
    *   **Leaders and elders:** Gu Yue Bo (Clan Leader, Rank 4), Gu Yue Chi Lian (Chi faction elder, Rank 3), Gu Yue Mo Chen (Mo faction elder, Rank 3), Academy Elder (Rank 3), Dark Hall Elder.
    *   **Ranks and strength:** Rank 4 is village lord level. Rank 3 is clan elder. Prominent Rank 2s (Qing Shu, Chi Shan, Mo Yan) lead 5-man mission groups (ch 81).
    *   **Territory and resources:** Controls the mountain waist and several small mortal villages at the mountain base. Operates the academy to train youth. Subsidizes top students with primeval stones (ch 53, 65, 72).
    *   **Internal running:** Highly political. Factions compete to recruit talented youths. Exams dictate student futures (mid-year and year-end arena fights). Graduating at 16 means moving out, forming 5-man mission groups, and inheriting family fortunes. Class chairman/vice-chairman roles are used to integrate students into the system via rewards and status (ch 49, 53, 78, 81, 82).
*   **Jia Family:** A wealthy merchant clan controlling an entire mountain. Run by a Rank 5 father. Sons Jia Fu and Jia Gui (both Rank 4) are locked in a bitter succession dispute, leading separate caravans to compete for assets (ch 45, 55, 56).
*   **Bai Clan & Xiong Clan:** Rival clans on Qing Mao Mountain. Bai clan is at the back mountain waterfall; Xiong clan is at the fore mountain. Bai clan is rising fast due to a Rank 3 A-grade genius, Bai Ning Bing. They are suspected of assassinating Gu Yue talents (ch 65, 74, 76).

## 4. People
*   **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** Protagonist, C-grade talent. Cold, calculating, ruthless loner. Reaches Rank 1 peak stage. Uses Moonlight, Liquor worm, Little Light, White Boar, and Jade Skin Gu. Kills Jia Jin Sheng, Wang Er, and the Wang family. Finds the Flower Wine Monk's tunnel, hunts boars bare-handed, and brutally crushes his brother in the year-end exam to claim first place (ch 44-86).
*   **Gu Yue Fang Zheng:** A-grade talent, twin brother of Fang Yuan. Backed by Gu Yue Bo. Reaches Rank 2 initial stage. Uses Moonlight, Bronze Skin, and Jade Skin Gu. Gets poisoned by an assassin but survives. Displays massive confidence in the arena, crushing Mo Bei and Chi Cheng, but gets brutally beaten and humiliated by Fang Yuan (ch 48, 50, 63, 72, 75, 83-85).
*   **Jia Jin Sheng:** D-grade talent, Jia family son. Runs a gambling den. Tricked by Fang Yuan into entering the secret cave, where he plans to kill Fang Yuan for the legacy but is instantly decapitated by Fang Yuan's moonblade (ch 44-46).
*   **Jia Fu:** Rank 4 merchant leader. Fat, short, with a reputation for fairness. Interrogates Fang Yuan over his brother's disappearance but clears him due to the Bamboo Gentleman Gu. Suspects his rival Jia Gui (ch 45, 55-57, 59).
*   **Gu Yue Bo:** Rank 4 Clan Leader. Wise and politically astute. Personally mentors Fang Zheng to break his inferiority complex. Tolerates Fang Yuan's rebelliousness to use him as a training hurdle for the other youths (ch 55, 58, 63, 78, 86).
*   **Academy Elder:** Rank 3. Strict and impartial on the surface, but internally hates Fang Yuan's defiance. Fails to suppress him due to Fang Yuan's manipulation of rules. Heals Fang Yuan post-exam to probe his aperture (ch 49, 51-53, 86).
*   **Gu Yue Mo Bei:** B-grade talent, Mo faction heir. Uses Yellow Camel Longhorn Beetle Gu for stamina. Frequently loses to Fang Yuan and Fang Zheng (ch 48, 54, 62, 82).
*   **Gu Yue Chi Cheng:** C-grade talent, Chi faction heir. Uses Dragonpill cricket Gu to evade. Receives secret primeval essence transfers from his grandfather Chi Lian. Arrogant but cowardly in close combat (ch 50, 62, 83).
*   **Wang Da:** Demonic Rank 2 Gu Master. Former hunter who killed his wife to refine the Love Life Separation Gu. Hides in the forest using Shadow Follower Gu. Attempts to assassinate Fang Yuan out of revenge, mistakenly attacks Fang Zheng, and is killed by clan elders (ch 74-76).
*   **Jiang He:** Rank 2 Gu Master stationed at the mortal village. Discovers Fang Yuan's murder of the Wang family but covers it up to protect his own job evaluation, inadvertently becoming Fang Yuan's political cover (ch 69, 78).
*   **Gu Yue Qing Shu, Chi Shan, Mo Yan:** Prominent Rank 2 group leaders. Qing Shu is mild-mannered (green hair). Chi Shan is huge, red-muscled, and known for immense natural strength. Mo Yan is Mo Bei's fiercely protective sister (ch 81, 82).

## 5. Gu and cultivation
*   **Cultivation mechanics (Rank 1-3):** Rank 1 essence changes color with stage: Initial (jade green), Middle (pale green), Upper (dark green), Peak (black green). Higher stage essence is more concentrated and durable (e.g., peak essence is twice as effective as upper). Cultivation involves using essence to continuously batter the aperture walls (light walls turn to water walls at middle stage, crystal walls at peak stage). Primeval stones are drained to rapidly recover essence (ch 50, 80).
*   **Money and prices:** Primeval stones are the currency. Top-tier Rank 1 Gu (Black/White Boar, Yellow Camel Longhorn Beetle, Liquor Worm) cost ~600 stones. Fang Yuan extorts ~68 stones per session but burns ~5 stones a day feeding his 6 Gu (ch 45, 54, 64).
*   **Liquor worm:** Rank 1. Refines primeval essence by one minor realm. Eats monkey wine (ch 44).
*   **White Boar Gu:** Rank 1. Milky white ladybug shape. Permanently increases a Gu Master's physical strength up to one boar's strength. Eats massive amounts of pork (ch 63, 64, 70).
*   **Jade Skin Gu:** Rank 1. Flat, green, bedbug-like. Provides top-tier defense by forming a green light barrier. Eats jade rocks (or jade eye stone monkey eyes) (ch 63, 79).
*   **Little Light Gu:** Rank 1. Pentagram shape. Doubles the size and range of the Moonlight Gu. Combined cost is highly efficient (ch 62, 70).
*   **Earth Treasury Flower Gu:** Rank 1. Consumable. Planted in soil, it feeds on earth energy and suspends a stored Gu worm in golden nectar hibernation (ch 63, 79).
*   **Shadow Follower Gu:** Rank 2. Turns the user into a dark shadow for stealth. Countered by bright light (ch 74, 76).
*   **Love Life Separation Gu:** Rank 2. The #1 poison Gu. Purple nails. Requires a lover's heart to refine (ch 74, 76).
*   **Flash Blink Gu:** Rank 1. Consumable. Explodes into blinding white light (ch 76).
*   **Footprint Gu:** Rank 1. Translucent yogurt-like foot. Explodes into powder that highlights the user's last 30,000 footprints (ch 57).
*   **Bamboo Gentleman:** Rank 4. Emerald green stick-bug shape. Detects lies. Must be fed honesty; dies if the user lies during refinement (ch 57).
*   **Other Gu:** Photo-audio Gu (records images/sound, ch 44), Innocent Mushroom (cleans air, ch 44), Crystal Ladybug (stores liquid, ch 44), One-stretch Golden Light Worm / Green Silk Gu / Water Spider Gu (defensive/raincoats, ch 47), Bronze / Iron / Stone / Beast Skin Gu (defense, ch 62), Brute Force / Yellow Camel Longhorn Beetle Gu (strength/stamina, ch 62), Pink / Flower / Black Boar Gu (weight/strength, ch 62), Relic Gu (boosts cultivation by one realm, ch 53).

## 6. Creatures and nature
*   **Wild Boar:** Ferocious, omnivorous, with thick black/grey fur and sharp tusks. Fast but can only charge in straight lines. Extremely tough (ch 65, 70).
*   **Wild Boar King:** Elephant-sized, highly dangerous beast living deep in the red cross zone. Hosts wild Gu worms in its body (ch 77).
*   **Jade Eye Stone Monkey:** Lives in colonies inside the dark holes of the underground stone pillars. Extremely agile. When killed, their bodies turn to stone and shatter, leaving behind two heavy jade pearls (eyes) (ch 79).
*   **Wandering Old Wolf:** A crippled, brown, one-eyed wolf. Highly alert and cunning. Retreats immediately when it senses it is outmatched (ch 80).
*   **Thousand Li Earthwolf spider:** Rank 5 steed Gu used by the Flower Wine Monk to burrow tunnels (ch 46).
*   **Caravan Beasts:** Ostrich chickens, fat beetle worms, shaggy mountain spiders, winged snakes, and Treasure Brass Toads used for transport (ch 44, 47, 59).

## 7. Events and conflicts
*   **Murder of Jia Jin Sheng:** Secret Cave. Fang Yuan tricks Jia Jin Sheng to see the image wall. The wall activates, demanding the inheritor destroy the Gu Yue clan. Jia plans to kill Fang Yuan for the legacy, but Fang Yuan instantly decapitates him with a moonblade. Fang Yuan takes the legacy and shifts blame to Jia Gui (ch 46, 56).
*   **Guards incident:** Academy classroom. Two guards disturb Fang Yuan's breakthrough. Fang Yuan severs their limbs, arrives at class covered in blood, and uses his middle-stage breakthrough to legally justify his actions. The Elder is forced to praise him (ch 51-52).
*   **Interrogation by Jia Fu:** Clan head pavilion. Jia Fu uses the Bamboo Gentleman to interrogate Fang Yuan. Fang Yuan uses the hidden Rank 6 Spring Autumn Cicada to suppress the Bamboo Gentleman, allowing him to lie flawlessly and clear his name (ch 55-57).
*   **Hunting the Wang Family:** Mountain foot wooden house. Fang Yuan kills Wang Er (a highly skilled mortal hunter) with moonblades after using the sun to blind him. He forces old man Wang to draw a map, then slaughters him and his daughter to secure the authentic hidden beast skin map. Gu Master Jiang He covers it up (ch 65-69).
*   **Mid-year exam & Assassination:** Mountain forest. Students hunt boars. Fang Yuan evades assassination by walking toward the Boar King's territory. Wang Da mistakenly attacks Fang Zheng, killing three hidden Rank 2 elders before being killed. Fang Zheng survives via Jade Skin Gu but gets poisoned. Fang Yuan wins 1st place by turning in previously hunted tusks (ch 73-77).
*   **Year-end exam finals:** Academy arena. Fang Zheng defeats Mo Bei and Chi Cheng using his Rank 2 cultivation and Jade Skin Gu. In the finals against Fang Yuan, Fang Yuan easily reads his moves, beats him down, and repeatedly stomps his head into the floor. When Fang Zheng triggers his Jade Skin Gu in a hot-blooded resurgence, Fang Yuan ruthlessly shatters the jade barrier with his bare, bloody fists, winning 1st place (ch 82-85).

## 8. Mood and time
*   **Season and weather:** Batch begins in late Spring with heavy, bleak rain (ch 47). Moves through a hot, bright Summer (ch 48, 72), into Autumn with red leaves and cold winds (ch 80), and ends in early Winter with light snow during the year-end exam (ch 81, 82).
*   **Time of day:** Fang Yuan operates primarily at night, returning to his hostel at midnight after exploring the secret cave or hunting, and sleeping through daytime classes.
*   **Tone:** Bleak, ruthless, and highly pragmatic. The narrative emphasizes that "life is as cheap as grass," survival of the fittest, and the extreme lengths required to walk the "demonic path."

## 9. Recurring objects
*   **Flower Wine Monk's image wall:** A rock face playing a holographic loop of the monk fighting the 4th Gen leader. Shaved down to reveal a tunnel (ch 46, 60).
*   **Wang Family Beast Skin Map:** A heavy, white beast skin map detailing the mountains, traps, boar herds, three red circles (Wang Da's hideouts), and one red cross (boar king) (ch 68, 73).
*   **Wild Boar Tusks:** Hunted by Fang Yuan and hoarded in the secret cave, later used to win the mid-year exam without expending effort on the day (ch 71, 77).
