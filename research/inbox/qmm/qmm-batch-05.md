## Places and layout
*   **Gu Yue Underground Cavern**: Sized like the village square, acting as the Gu storage vault where elders pick Gu worms (ch 160). It contains a deeper restricted area holding the official clan historical records (ch 178) and a secret room for viewing ritual recordings (ch 179).
*   **Centipede Cave (Third secret room)**: Accessed past the rock monkey area. It is a rocky corridor about three meters tall and two meters wide, branching into many narrow paths containing swarms of centipedes (ch 162).
*   **Flower Wine Monk's Escape Tunnel**: A long, sloping underground path dug by a giant spider Gu. It is over three Li long (1.5 km), taking hours to walk as it leads deep underground (ch 162).
*   **Natural Essence Spring Area**: A deep underground cavity holding the village's natural spirit spring. The spring water is ash-gray and flows continually in a spiral "like a mini tornado" (ch 162). It is enclosed behind a phantom water-crystal wall created by a Moat Gu that lets objects pass through it (ch 163).
*   **Mo Faction Courtyard Cooling Pavilion**: A quiet, graceful outdoor pavilion attached to the Mo faction estate, featuring abundant plants, an artificial mountain, and a crystal-clear spring (ch 172). 
*   **Bai Clan Spirit Spring Restricted Area**: An underground cavern deep below the Bai village housing their natural spirit spring in a deep pool, holding a sacred white snake Gu (ch 173).
*   **Blood Pond Graveyard**: A massive, hidden cavern located directly below the Gu Yue spirit spring. It features a vast lake of literal bright red blood, with glowing, soft red soil walls that provide a dim light. Hundreds of cracks in the high ceiling let in water and aquatic creatures, which immediately drop into the lake and are drained into floating skeletons. The area contains a ruined escape tunnel dug by the Flower Wine Monk. It is the secret resting place of the first generation clan leader (ch 182).
*   **Three-Clan Tournament Zone**: A 100-li (50 km) wilderness radius around the mountain used as a battleground for young Gu masters, lasting until sunset (ch 186).

## Factions and power
*   **Gu Yue Clan**: Severely weakened by the wolf tide, losing over half their elders (ch 171). The Chi faction is falling, and the Mo faction is near collapse after losing its heir. Elder Mo Chen drops to Rank 2 due to injuries and tries to recruit Fang Yuan to save his faction (ch 172). The Medicine faction, having taken fewer casualties, attempts to cleanly absorb the weakened factions to increase their power (ch 178).
*   **Bai Clan**: They are pushing hard to surpass Gu Yue, relying almost entirely on Bai Ning Bing to eliminate the future generations of the other clans (ch 189).
*   **Xiong Clan**: Temporarily presumed destroyed but revealed to have preserved most of their strength by having their people retreat and hide early using an ancestral Gu (ch 175). They plot to let the other clans be exhausted by the wolves, making them a serious threat again (ch 175). They establish a temporary alliance with Gu Yue in the tournament to take down Bai Ning Bing (ch 189).

## People
*   **Gu Yue Fang Yuan**: Now a Rank 3 clan elder, he feigns political retreat by openly admitting cowardice in battle (ch 171). He extorts peers for primeval stones (ch 161), eventually extracting the precious lotus from the spirit spring, which ruins the village's foundation and sends him plummeting into the blood pond (ch 180). He uses a Gu to force his cultivation to Rank 3 peak stage to survive the blood crypts (ch 187), abandoning poisoned Gu to escape on a giant spider mount and dueling Bai Ning Bing (ch 190).
*   **Gu Yue Fang Zheng**: Rank 2/3. He acts as Tie Ruo Nan's assistant. Upon realizing that Fang Yuan faked his early decline and drunkard persona as part of a deep scheme, Fang Zheng suffers a profound psychological breakdown. His world collapses out of intense inferiority, but he rebounds with a deranged determination to kill his brother for breaking the law (ch 181).
*   **Bai Ning Bing**: Rank 3 Bai clan genius. He has completely embraced his impending death from the "Northern Dark Ice Soul physique," resolving to find his "path" by witnessing the brilliance of the world (ch 169). His physique completely heals fatal injuries as his body turns to ice (ch 178). He slaughters over a hundred allied Gu masters during the tournament until fighting Fang Yuan (ch 189).
*   **Tie Xue Leng**: The "Divine Investigator," a Rank 5 Gu Master from the Southern Border. He wears a copper mask, stands straight, and enforces justice mercilessly (ch 170). Despite heavy injuries, he fights the myriad beast king and later plunges into the blood pond graveyard to stop the first generation ancestor's blood calamity (ch 184).
*   **Tie Ruo Nan**: Tie Xue Leng's daughter and a Rank 3 Gu master. She has a tomboyish appearance, dark complexion, and rigid ideals of equality and justice (ch 170). She deduces Fang Yuan's crimes and wrongly concludes he possesses a legendary ten extreme physique because of his extreme lack of jealousy toward his brother (ch 181). 
*   **First Gen Gu Yue**: The founder of the Gu Yue village, a former demonic cultivator (ch 178). He secretly buried himself in the blood crypts underneath the village, turning into a Rank 5 "Blood Wight Gu" (a flying zombie) to live forever. He awakens to reclaim his blood legacy (ch 185).
*   **Elderly White-Browed Gu Master**: A mysterious new character riding a giant crane. He appears in the sky holding a tracking blood Gu, hunting down his "senior brother" (the First Gen Gu Yue) to steal back an opportunity (ch 190).
*   **Gu Yue Mo Chen**: A Gu Yue elder whose heir died. He actively sacrifices his own reputation to cover for Fang Yuan, attempting to bribe and force him into marrying his granddaughter, Gu Yue Mo Yan, to save their faction's legacy (ch 172). 
*   **Gu Yue Yao Ji**: Medicine faction leader. She intentionally broke her own arm to avoid dying in the wolf battle (ch 171) and plots with Tie Ruo Nan to ruin Fang Yuan's standing and destroy the Mo faction (ch 178).

## Gu and cultivation
*   **Cultivation and breakthroughs**: Rank 3 peak stage produces "snow silver" primeval essence, highly sustainable and strong (ch 187). "Ten extreme physiques" represent ultimate talent that defies heaven, automatically gaining endless potential but ensuring an early explosive death (ch 181). 
*   **Spring Autumn Cicada**: Rank 6. It recovers too fast, threatening to burst Fang Yuan's weak aperture (ch 177). He realizes it cannot be moved out of the body once refined as a vital Gu, forcing him to keep it inside or self-destruct to use it (ch 186).
*   **Blood Moon Gu**: Rank 3. Refined by Fang Yuan. Shoots blood-red moonblades that inflict unhealable bleeding injuries (ch 159). Weakness: oozes blood and drops in power a few days a month (ch 160).
*   **Tusita Flower**: Rank 3. A storage grass Gu resembling a red lantern with three green leaves. Resides as a tattoo on the tongue, glows red, and swallows up to ~15,000 primeval stones (ch 161).
*   **Chainsaw Golden Centipede**: Rank 3. Metre-long, dark-gold insect with silver sawteeth on its sides. Subdued by the Spring Autumn Cicada's aura (ch 162). Fang Yuan wraps it around his arm like a hiltless chainsaw-sword (ch 166).
*   **Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus**: Rank 3 flower Gu (advanceable to Rank 6) created by immortal Genesis Lotus. It acts as a portable spirit spring that produces natural primeval essence inside the aperture. Refining it completely ruins the natural essence spring where it grew (ch 163).
*   **Blood Guillotine**: Rank 5. Fingertip-sized red locusts with scary faces. They suck a Gu master's origin blood to multiply exponentially into a storm of red death (ch 165).
*   **Thunder Shield Gu**: A defensive Gu with a light blue glass turtle-like shell. Forms a semicircle eerie blue lightning shield. Captured and refined by Fang Yuan, but destroyed shortly after (ch 167).
*   **Living Steel Gu**: Rank 2. Looks like hole-filled black charcoal. Burnt up to produce black steel smoke that rapidly heals the damaged razor teeth of the Chainsaw Golden Centipede (ch 177). 
*   **Stone Aperture Gu**: A grey dice-shaped consumable Gu. It crushes into smoke that turns aperture walls into extreme, impenetrable stone, instantly skyrocketing cultivation to peak stage but permanently destroying any future rank progression (ch 187).
*   **Thousand Li Earthwolf Spider**: Rank 5 steed Gu. Pitch black and metallic with three pairs of spiral, drill-like legs. Eats soil and drains massive amounts of primeval essence (ch 188).
*   **Righteous Gu**: Emits an invisible, mountain-like aura that severely weakens the combat resolve of those with vile minds or weak willpower (ch 170).
*   **Giant Mountain Puppet Gu**: Used by Tie Xue Leng. Turns his copper mask into an 18-meter-tall giant formed of converged mountain rocks and soil (ch 184).
*   **Heaven Earth Magnificent Sound Gu**: Rank 5. Releases a world-shaking soundwave that can physically decimate hordes of bats and flying insects (ch 186).
*   **Ironfist Grappling Gu**, **Oil Dragon Gu**, **Fire Dragon Gu**: Used by Tie Xue Leng. Create massive iron hands and elemental dragons to roast giant monsters (ch 184).
*   **Cactus Pointer**: Rank 3 consumable. A planted seed that rapidly grows into a cactus, blooming a white flower that unfolds into a single word hint based on the user's subconscious clues (ch 176).
*   **White Form Immortal Snake Gu**: Rank 5. Snowy scales, jade eyes, celestial whiskers. It feeds on spirit spring water. Enters Bai Ning Bing due to his ice physique, shielding him by spitting fog that obscures vision (ch 173).
*   **Blood Wight Gu**: Rank 5. Transforms the user into a flying zombie, granting immortality but killing the aperture's ability to naturally generate primeval essence (ch 185).
*   **Bladewing Blood Bat Gu**: Rank 3. Wing edges act as steel blades. Hundreds are coordinated entirely by a single male bat in the swarm (ch 187).
*   **Blood Frenzy Gu**: Rank 4. Formless blood essence that infects other Gu, allowing them to absorb natural essence but quickly causing them to lose control and melt into a pool of contaminated blood (ch 183).

## Creatures and nature
*   **Thunder Crown Wolf**: Myriad beast king. Tall as a small hill, eerie blue scales, golden fur, and a towering stiff hair crown. Highly resistant, emits thick lightning sweeps, handles coordinated attacks from multiple elders (ch 163).
*   **Lightning Lycan**: Myriad beast king. Highly cunning with human-level intelligence. Resembles the thunder crown wolf but has shriveled forelegs, meaning it bounds on its hind legs (ch 168). It organizes wolf packs defensively to trap humans.
*   **Blood River Python**: An enormous swamp python over forty meters long living in the blood lake. Has red scales, gold bone spikes over its eyes, and regenerates extremely fast when submerged in blood (ch 183).

## Events and conflicts
*   **Battle with the Thunder Crown Wolf**: Outside Gu Yue village. Clan leader and elders use combo attacks to wound it, eventually sacrificing dozens of injured villagers to summon the Blood Guillotine. The wolf escapes, severely hurt, resulting in an ambush by Bai Ning Bing (ch 164). 
*   **Second Battle against Bai Ning Bing**: In a valley. Fang Yuan uses the Chainsaw Golden Centipede to ruthlessly butcher Bai reinforcements. Bai Ning Bing uses an unstoppable Iceblade storm that eventually crushes the dying Thunder Crown Wolf, destroying its loot. Both clans take heavy casualties (ch 166).
*   **The Lycan Ambush**: Entering the smoke cast by the Lightning Lycan, Fang Yuan and Bai Ning Bing navigate using synchronized auditory and visual Gu skills to escape the deadly trap. Tie Xue Leng drives the beast away with Righteous Gu and Ironfist Grappling Gu (ch 168).
*   **The Investigation of Tie Ruo Nan**: Ruo Nan interrogates villagers and uses the Cactus Pointer and visual recordings. She exposes that Fang Yuan killed an innocent family, bought wine to hide his Liquor Worm, and wrongly deduces he has a mythical Extreme Physique to explain his lack of emotional reactions (ch 176). 
*   **The Blood Crypt Battle**: Tie Xue Leng discovers the underground graveyard and fights the Blood River Python using a giant rock puppet and elemental dragons. The First Gen Gu Yue awakens as a zombie and retaliates with legions of blood bats. Fang Yuan escapes the fallout using an ancient spider Gu (ch 184).
*   **The Three-Clan Tournament**: Held in the wilderness surrounding the mountain. The Xiong and Gu Yue clans gang up entirely on Bai Ning Bing. Bai Ning Bing massacres them all, beheading and freezing numerous young elites, before Fang Yuan bursts from the ground and they duel on their Rank 5 mounts (ch 189).

## Mood and time
*   Moving from late summer into early autumn, the weather is cooler and moonlit. The mood shifts from violent desperation during the wolf tide into bleak, ruthless paranoia as political assassinations, intense investigations, and ancient blood magics emerge. 

## Recurring objects
*   **Bamboo Wine**: Used repeatedly by Fang Yuan in the past, seemingly pointlessly, which Tie Ruo Nan uses as the primary evidence to prove he was secretly feeding the Liquor worm (ch 177). 
*   **Photo-audio Gu**: An item that is crushed into seven-color smoke to playback perfect audio and visual illusions of the past, used by the village to record the Awakening Ceremony (ch 179).
