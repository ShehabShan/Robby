# QMM notes ch 201–201

1. **Chapter map:**
ch 201: resting at the beach after escaping Qing Mao mountain | Jiang mountain shallow beach | no

2. **Places and layout:**
- **Jiang mountain shallow beach:** A small shoreline beside a river with sandy water. It is bordered on three sides by a tall, slippery cliff. The top of the cliff is covered in dense vegetation and a forest, but the beach itself is barren of wood. Fang Yuan chose it as a resting spot because the cliffs protect against most wild beasts. (ch 201)
- **Bai Gu mountain:** Mentioned by Fang Yuan as their next travel destination. (ch 201)
- **Qing Mao mountain:** The site of Fang Yuan's rebirth and the blood barrier slaughter, which they escaped five days prior. (ch 201)
- **The river:** The waterway they floated down on a raft for five days. Its water contains sand. (ch 201)

3. **Factions and power:**
- **Gu Yue, Xiong, and Bai clans:** None of the clans are active here. Bai Ning Bing is noted as the former Bai clan genius, but she is currently stranded without her clan. (ch 201)

4. **People:**
- **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** Rank 1 initial stage. Key Gu: Tusita flower, Yang Gu, Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus. Pragmatic, highly prepared, and calm. He possesses an A-grade primeval essence recovery speed. He heals his severe wounds using mortal medicine, cooks a meal, and temporarily lends his high-rank Gu to Bai Ning Bing since his cultivation is too low to use them effectively. (ch 201)
- **Bai Ning Bing:** Former Bai clan genius. Rank 3 cultivation, but currently has no Gu worms of her own. Key Gu: temporarily commands Chainsaw Golden Centipede and Sky Canopy Gu. Arrogant, prideful, and deeply frustrated by being transformed into a woman. She reluctantly follows Fang Yuan's lead and accepts his lent Gu to regain some combat strength. (ch 201)

5. **Gu and cultivation:**
- **Tusita flower:** A Gu resembling a red lantern flower. It rotates, floats in mid-air, and glows red. Used as a storage Gu to hold large amounts of mortal supplies like medicine, clothes, coal, and cooking gear. Used by Fang Yuan. (ch 201)
- **Yin Yang Rotation Gu:** A paired set of Gu. If one of the pair is destroyed, the other permanently loses its effect, meaning Bai Ning Bing would never be able to return to being male. (ch 201)
- **Yang Gu:** Refined by Fang Yuan and held in his aperture. It can be detonated instantly at his command. Bai Ning Bing needs it to reverse her gender, but Fang Yuan refuses to hand it over until he reaches Rank 3. (ch 201)
- **Chainsaw Golden Centipede:** Rank 3 Gu. Lent to Bai Ning Bing. (ch 201)
- **Sky Canopy Gu:** Rank 3 Gu. Lent to Bai Ning Bing. (ch 201)
- **Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus:** Held by Fang Yuan. (ch 201)
- **Healing Gu:** None are present. The ones looted from the battle on Qing Mao mountain starved to death over the five-day river journey. (ch 201)
- **Cultivation mechanics:** A Rank 1 initial stage Gu Master cannot properly wield Rank 3 Gu worms, even if they have A-grade recovery and a Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus. (ch 201)
- **Lending Gu:** Gu Masters can temporarily lend their Gu to others. The borrower can communicate with the Gu's internal will and use its powers, though not as smoothly as a personally refined Gu. The original owner can instantly revoke access with a single thought. (ch 201)

6. **Creatures and nature:**
- **Wild beasts and wild Gu:** Implied to live in the dense forest on top of the cliff. Fang Yuan warns that they might ambush them, and their meat could be poisonous, which makes hunting risky without a healing Gu. (ch 201)

7. **Events and conflicts:**
- **Resting at the shallow beach:** After floating on a raft for five days following the destruction at Qing Mao mountain, Fang Yuan and Bai Ning Bing land on a beach. Fang Yuan treats his wounds with mortal medicine paste, gives Bai Ning Bing new clothes, and cooks dried jerky using coal stones. Recognizing his Rank 1 limitations, Fang Yuan lends his Rank 3 Gu worms to Bai Ning Bing to serve as their defense. Takes place at the Jiang mountain beach. (ch 201)

8. **Mood and time:**
- **Time of day:** Late in the afternoon or evening, as it is getting dark and they prepare to keep watch in shifts for the night. Exactly five days after the escape from Qing Mao mountain. (ch 201)
- **Mood:** Pragmatic, grounded, and tense. There is a strong feeling of starting over from scratch, emphasized by the reliance on mundane tools rather than magical Gu. (ch 201)

9. **Recurring objects:**
- **Mortal supplies:** Fang Yuan prepared extensively, storing bandages, disinfectant powder, blackish-green blood-clotting paste, spare clothes, coal stones, kerosene, flint, a metal rack, an iron pot, and jerky inside the Tusita flower. (ch 201)
