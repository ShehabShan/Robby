# 2. Places and layout
*   **Qing Mao Mountain nature:** During winter, it is covered in snow, featuring naked branches alongside evergreen pines and "Qing Mao bamboo" (ch 87). In spring, wild flowers bloom on sunny hills, and creatures like "dragonpill crickets" and "peacock parrots" appear (ch 109). Autumn turns the forest leaves dark, yellow, and red (ch 119).
*   **Small valley:** A location on the mountain containing a frozen swamp where teams collect "permafrost decayed soil" (ch 88).
*   **Underground apartments:** A cheap rental housing option in the village with terrible airflow and dampness, making tenants prone to illness (ch 89).
*   **Fang Yuan's rented bamboo building:** A cheap, worn-out building where rent is paid daily. FY rents a second-floor room with noisy old floors. It initially contains only one bed, a torn blanket leaking yellow cotton, and an oil lamp (ch 90, 91).
*   **Hamlet at the mountain foot:** A small settlement where Jiang He lives. FY hides here for five days to safely breakthrough to Rank 2 (ch 92).
*   **Internal affairs hall interrogation room:** A dark room containing a long table for the elders and a single chair for the interrogated person, illuminated by a pillar of sunlight from the lone window (ch 97).
*   **Wine tavern:** Part of FY's inherited property. It is located on the eastern side near the eastern gate, an area with high foot traffic. It consists of one floor and an underground wine cellar. The interior has a black tiled floor, eight square tables with four benches each, and a dark brown sales counter equipped with an abacus, brushes, and paper. Behind the counter is a liquor cabinet displaying black pottery jars and bright porcelain bottles. In the summer, sheds are set up outside for extra seating (ch 103).
*   **Three bamboo buildings:** The residential property FY inherits, fully rented out to struggling female Rank 2 Gu Masters (ch 103).
*   **Garden arena stage:** Located at Gu Yue Qing Shu's residence, used for practicing Gu abilities (ch 104).
*   **Caravan camp:** Set up in the village during spring, consisting of a continuous stretch of tents (ch 109).
*   **'Three star cave' treehouse:** A Rank 3 Gu house used by the caravan as a secure shop. It is an 18-meter-tall tree with a 10-meter-diameter base. The brown trunk has three layers of internal space, with windows letting in sunlight. The roots and branches twist like dragons. The first floor sells Rank 1 Gu, the second floor Rank 2, and the third floor Rank 3. The interior features wooden counters and seating. Behind a leaf-covered wall on the third floor is a hidden whirling staircase leading to a small study room (ch 109, 111, 113).
*   **Underground rock forest (central area):** Exploring deeper reveals the largest, thickest stone pillar extending from the ceiling almost to the ground. It houses over 500 stone monkeys. At the top of this pillar is a large cave, twice the size of the others, belonging to the monkey king (ch 114). Below the central pillar is a small cave containing rock stairs that lead down into darkness (ch 116).
*   **Huang Long River (Yellow Dragon River) / Mountain foot:** A river channel at the base of Qing Mao Mountain, flanked by paddy fields and vegetable fields. The riverbed becomes wider downstream (ch 117, 119, 120). 

# 3. Factions and power
*   **Gu Yue Clan:**
    *   *Leaders/Elders:* Gu Yue Bo (clan leader, Rank 4), Gu Yue Chi Lian (Chi faction elder), Gu Yue Mo Chen (Mo faction elder), Gu Yue Yao Ji (medicine hall elder, Rank 3, uses a walking cane and holds immense influence), and the internal affairs and punishment hall elders (ch 110, 117).
    *   *Rules and Operations:* Gu Masters must complete at least one mission per month or their evaluations drop. Rank 1 Gu Masters cannot abandon missions, but Rank 2 can abandon one per year (ch 89). Inheriting family assets requires the claimant to be 16 years old with Rank 1 middle stage cultivation, and they must pass an asset mission applied for with no other active missions (ch 90). The Gu worms of dead clansmen are retrieved as inheritance for their successors (ch 96). Openly attacking a clansman in the village without a formal Gu battle is forbidden (ch 108).
    *   *Geopolitics:* The clan relies heavily on its underground spirit spring. They endure a wolf tide every three years (ch 93, 117).
*   **Jia Family Caravan:** Led by Rank 4 Gu Master Jia Fu, who rides a Treasure Brass Toad. The caravan brings a massive treehouse shop. They use a secret written bidding system to avoid offending the clansmen's interpersonal relationships. Jia Fu is in fierce competition with his brother Jia Gui for family assets (ch 109, 110).
*   **Bai Village:** An emerging power challenging the Gu Yue clan's dominance. They are famous for their "white grain liquid" and bitter shell wine (ch 117).

# 4. People
*   **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** Reaches Rank 2 middle stage. He inherits his parents' tavern and bamboo houses, then ruthlessly sells them back to his uncle to buy a red steel Relic Gu. He trains his combat skills against stone monkeys and uses river tides to push the River Swallowing Toad, earning a promotion to team leader and the title of clan hero (ch 92, 102, 112, 115, 120).
*   **Gu Yue Jiao San:** Known as the "sickly snake." He is a Rank 2 group leader with sickly yellow skin and triangular eyes. He conspires with Uncle Dong Tu to trap FY in continuous missions. FY suffocates him to death while he is paralyzed after a wolf attack (ch 87, 96).
*   **Gu Yue Kong Jing:** Rank 2 logistics support in Jiao San's group. He is eaten by lightning wolves (ch 88, 95).
*   **Hua Xing:** Female Gu Master in Jiao San's group. She is bitten in half by the wild boar king after FY intentionally lets go of its tusks (ch 94).
*   **Uncle Gu Yue Dong Tu:** Known as the "hidden elder," having retired at Rank 2 peak stage. He controls FY's inheritance and sells vitality leaves. He eventually buys back the tavern and houses from FY (ch 90, 101, 112).
*   **Aunt:** Haggard and shrewish, she loses her lavish lifestyle when FY claims his inheritance (ch 90, 102, 112).
*   **Gu Yue Chi Shan:** Rank 2 upper stage. A tall man with red skin and immense physical strength. A fair leader who protects his team. He recommends FY to push the River Swallowing Toad (ch 93, 118).
*   **Gu Yue Chi Cheng:** Rank 2, heir of the Chi faction. Arrogant and hostile toward FY (ch 93).
*   **Gu Yue Fang Zheng:** Rank 2, FY's brother. Challenges FY to a Gu battle to win back the assets for their aunt and uncle. Uses the Moon Raiment Gu (ch 104).
*   **Gu Yue Qing Shu:** Rank 2 peak stage, B grade talent ("false A grade"). A gentle and loyal member of the clan leader's faction. Uses green vine, pine needle, and Moonwhirl Gu. Tries to buy FY's Liquor worm but backs down when FY threatens to ruin FZ's reputation (ch 104, 105).
*   **Gu Yue Man Shi:** Rank 2 group leader with a thick black beard. Survived an encounter with Bai Ning Bing. Tries to extort FY's tavern but is brutally defeated and terrified by FY (ch 107, 108).
*   **Jia Fu:** Rank 4 caravan leader. Bears a facial scar from fighting his brother. Tries to recruit FY after noticing his bidding accuracy (ch 109, 113).
*   **Gu Yue Yao Ji:** Rank 3 medicine hall elder. A hunchback with a cane who dotes on her granddaughter Yao Le. Bids against FY for a Liquor worm (ch 110, 111).
*   **Tie Xue Leng:** A famous divine investigator invited by Jia Fu to solve Jia Jin Sheng's murder. Expected to arrive the year after next (ch 113).

# 5. Gu and cultivation
*   **Cultivation mechanics:** Rank 1 uses green copper essence, Rank 2 uses red steel essence. Breaking the aperture walls to rank up requires 55% essence, but FY forces it using primeval stones with his 44% capacity (ch 91). Fusing Gu requires parallel thinking to merge consciousnesses; having a vital Gu prevents death of the vital Gu upon failure (ch 98, 106).
*   **Gu Master battle attire:** Dark blue long sleeves and pants, bamboo wrappings and shoes, a blue headband, and a loose belt with a bronze plate indicating rank number and color (green for 1, red for 2, white for 3) (ch 87, 110).
*   **Large Belly Frog:** Rank 2 support. Has a golden back and a snow-white belly. Vomits and stores items but croaks loudly each time. Cannot store Gu or poison (ch 88).
*   **Snake Tongue Gu:** Rank 2 recon. Senses heat in a small radius. Easily interfered with and cannot detect cold-blooded creatures. Used by Chi She (ch 93).
*   **Scarlet Pill Cricket Gu:** Used by Chi Cheng to maintain running speed (ch 93).
*   **Red Lava Python:** Rank 2. Has a dark red rock body with high temperatures. Used by Jiao San (ch 94).
*   **Corrosion blood grass Gu:** Activated with red steel essence. Turns a purple vine insignia on the thumb into tentacles that enter a target's stomach (ch 94).
*   **White Jade Gu:** Rank 2 defense. A moist white jade pebble. Fused from White Boar Gu and Jade Skin Gu (adding a wild boar king's tusk increases success rate by 20%). Provides double the defense of Jade Skin (ch 99, 100).
*   **Moon Raiment:** Rank 2 defense. Fused from Moonlight and Jade Skin. Forms a light blue floating ribbon. Evolving it to Rank 5 results in the "Moonlight Treasure King Gu" (ch 98, 104).
*   **Moonglow Gu:** Rank 2 offense. Fused from Moonlight and two Little Light Gu. Looks like a water blue crystal crescent, twice the size of a Moonlight Gu. Has triple the attack power and a 10m range. Costs 10% red steel essence per shot (ch 106, 114).
*   **Moon Scar Gu:** Rank 2 (Moonlight + Stone Scar), extends attack range to 20m (ch 106).
*   **Moonwhirl Gu:** Rank 2 (Moonlight + Whirlwind), shoots curved green moonblades (ch 104).
*   **Nine Leaf Vitality Grass:** Rank 2 healing. Has emerald green roots and 9 jade green leaves. Torn leaves become one-time-use Rank 1 vitality leaves. Regrowing a leaf costs 20% Rank 2 initial essence (ch 102, 106).
*   **Four Flavors Liquor worm:** Rank 2. Fused from 2 Liquor worms and 4 wines (sour, sweet, bitter, spicy). Refines primeval essence (ch 105).
*   **Monolith Gu:** Creates a deep grey rock skin defense. Used by Man Shi (ch 108).
*   **Cleansing Water Gu:** Light-blue leech shape. Removes mixed aura from an aperture. Sold for ~650 stones (ch 111).
*   **Black Boar Gu:** Rank 1. Increases fundamental strength and stacks with the White Boar Gu (ch 111).
*   **Relic Gu series:** Directly raises cultivation by a small realm. Green copper (Rank 1), red steel (Rank 2, costs 3k+ stones), white silver (Rank 3, costs ~50k stones), yellow golden (Rank 4) (ch 111, 115).
*   **Revert Gu:** Decomposes a Rank 2 Gu back into its Rank 1 components. A flat black stone with a blinking white eye pattern (ch 113).
*   **Stealth Rock Gu:** Rank 1. Grants invisibility but cannot hide blood. Looks like an uneven grey rock. Dropped by the monkey king (ch 116).
*   **River Swallowing Toad:** Rank 5. Hill-sized, with a snow-white belly and a clear sky blue back. Sleeps in rivers, swallows water, and vomits seafood. Has a mild temperament and only attacks if severely provoked (ch 117, 120).
*   **Money and Prices:** Nice rental rooms cost ~25 primeval stones/month. Mission rewards are usually 1-2 stones (ch 88). Rank 1 vitality leaves sell for 50-55 stones (ch 107), allowing FY to earn ~400 stones per batch of 9 (ch 106). The treehouse discounts the Liquor worm at 500 stones (market price 580) (ch 110). 

# 6. Creatures and nature
*   **Permafrost decayed soil:** Purple-black frozen swamp soil. Smelly but fertile, serves as food for the "Smelly Fart Fatworm" (ch 88).
*   **Wild Beasts:** Includes wild deer, turtleback apes, and white tigers (ch 93).
*   **Wild Boar King:** A thousand beast king. Elephant-sized with snow-white tusks (ch 94).
*   **Lightning wolves:** Their bites carry electric currents that cause cardiac arrest. Small beast hordes form when the wolf king expels old and injured wolves (ch 95, 96).
*   **Jade Eye Stone Monkeys:** Agile monkeys living in the underground rock pillars. They turn to stone and shatter when killed. The monkey king is a hundred beast king, three times larger with red eyes, and possesses the Stealth Rock Gu (ch 100, 114).
*   **Golden bees:** Fist-sized with gold and black stripes and sharp stingers. Large hives produce honey wine (ch 98).
*   **Hundred year bitter shell:** A black deep-pond conch with white rings. It swallows sand to form bitter water, which naturally ages into bitter shell wine (ch 117, 120).

# 7. Events and conflicts
*   **Jiao San group sabotage:** During a fight with a wild boar king, FY intentionally drops the boar's tusks, getting Hua Xing killed. He then sabotages a steel web, letting the boar loose. A lightning wolf pack attacks, wiping out the rest of the group. FY hides in a boar stomach to survive, then suffocates a paralyzed Jiao San to ensure his death (ch 94-96).
*   **Inheritance acquisition:** FY completes a dangerous honey wine mission, outmaneuvers Uncle Dong Tu using clan rules, and successfully claims his parents' extensive assets (ch 101-102).
*   **Man Shi tavern fight:** Gu Yue Man Shi attempts to extort FY's tavern. FY attacks instantly without warning using his Moonglow Gu. He critically injures Man Shi, terrifying him with a cold, indifferent gaze, firmly establishing his reputation among Rank 2 Gu Masters (ch 107-108).
*   **Caravan Gu auction:** FY uses precise bidding tactics to win a Liquor worm out from under Yao Ji. He then buys a Black Boar Gu and a red steel Relic Gu, funding the massive purchase by selling his tavern and houses back to Uncle Dong Tu (ch 110-113).
*   **Monkey King fight:** FY uses the red steel Relic Gu to reach Rank 2 middle stage. He engages the monkey king, triggering chaos among the monkey groups. The monkey king sneak attacks repeatedly while invisible. FY abandons his defense to bait an attack, uses his shirt to blind the monkey, and critically injures it with a moonblade, eventually killing it for its Stealth Rock Gu (ch 115-116).
*   **River Swallowing Toad crisis:** A Rank 5 toad blocks the river, threatening to flood the village and trigger a premature wolf tide. After Chi Shan fails to move it, the elders order FY to try. FY cleverly uses the crashing river tides to help push the toad. The toad wakes up, drinks the river, vomits seafood (including bitter shells FY needs), and leaves. FY is declared a clan hero (ch 118-120).
*   **Tavern cover-up:** Gu Masters in the tavern who previously insulted FY slaughter the mortal clerks to silence them, terrified that their insults against the new clan hero will ruin their careers (ch 120).

# 8. Mood and time
*   **Time and Weather:** The batch begins in winter with heavy snow (ch 87). Spring soon arrives, melting the ice and bringing blooming flowers and warm winds (ch 107, 109). Summer passes in a flash (ch 116), leading into early autumn with cooling weather, colorful leaves, and golden paddy fields (ch 117, 119).
*   **Tone:** Ruthless, calculating, and bleak. FY's complete disregard for clan ties and the lives of mortals is heavily emphasized. This pragmatism is mirrored by other Gu Masters, who casually slaughter mortal clerks just to protect their own reputations.

# 9. Recurring objects
*   **Bullskin water sack:** Used by FY to collect and store the golden honey wine (ch 100).
*   **Spring Autumn Cicada:** FY's vital Gu continues its slow recovery. It saves his life by warning him of the monkey king's sneak attack (ch 99, 115).
*   **Bamboo papers:** Used for recording missions in the internal affairs hall and for secret bidding at the caravan treehouse (ch 102, 110).
