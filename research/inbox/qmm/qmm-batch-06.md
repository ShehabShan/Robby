## Places and layout
* **Gu Yue village:** Devastated by crane attacks and battling Rank 5 experts. Bamboo buildings collapse, the clan main pavilion is half destroyed, and the square is covered in blood water (ch 191, 192). Eventually entirely swallowed and frozen by a massive glacier (ch 195, 199).
* **Qing Mao Mountain:** Turned into a sinister, frozen wasteland. Covered from peak to bottom by a "thousand li glacier" created by Bai Ning Bing's self-detonation (ch 195). The entire ecosystem is frozen solid, dooming all trapped living beings to die within three to five days (ch 199). After a couple of days, the ice begins to melt slightly, forming clear streams (ch 199).
* **Yellow Dragon River:** The third largest river in the Southern Border, spanning over 8,000 kilometers (ch 200). It zigzags across over half the region, starting at Mount Huang Guo, passing mountains like Mount Xuan Ming, Mount Gui Bei, Qing Mao Mountain, Bai Gu Mountain, and Mount Lei Ci, before emptying into the ocean (ch 200). Many tributaries flow down from Qing Mao Mountain into the main river (ch 200). It is extremely violent, filled with raging waves, rapid currents, fog, and dangerous reefs (ch 200). Populated by fish, turtles, snakes, clams, and aggressive predators (ch 200). It features soft, shallow beaches where certain deep-water predators cannot swim (ch 200).

## Factions and power
* **Gu Yue, Xiong, and Bai clans:** Effectively annihilated. The three clan leaders are killed outside the village early in the crane attack (ch 191). The remaining elders and members retreat to the Gu Yue village, where they are trapped and systematically slaughtered to harvest their blood—either by First gen Gu Yue (ch 194) or by Fang Yuan and Bai Ning Bing in the second timeline (ch 198). Any survivors scattered on the mountain are frozen in the glacier (ch 199). 
* **Immortal Crane Sect:** A sect located in the Middle Continent. Hundreds of years ago, Lord Sky Crane and First gen Gu Yue belonged to this sect as the "Righteous Twin Cranes" (ch 192). Lord Sky Crane operates on behalf of the sect to retrieve a stolen inheritance (ch 192, 199).

## People
* **Gu Yue Fang Yuan:** Rank 3 peak stage Gu Master until his rebirth (ch 196). Pushed to the brink by two Rank 5s, he self-detonates to reverse time (ch 196). In the new timeline, he betrays First gen Gu Yue to steal his Gu (ch 197) and ruthlessly slaughters his own clan with Bai Ning Bing's help to harvest their blood (ch 198). This process increases his aptitude to 90% (A-grade), but the conflicting Gu effects destroy his stone aperture, reducing his cultivation back to Rank 1 initial stage while restoring his natural primeval recovery (ch 198). He manipulates Bai Ning Bing into following him as they escape the mountain (ch 199).
* **Bai Ning Bing:** Rank 3 genius of the Bai clan. Realizing his death is certain, he protects Fang Yuan and triggers his "Northern Dark Ice Soul physique" to freeze the entire mountain, killing himself but trapping Lord Sky Crane (ch 195, 199). Fang Yuan resurrects him, which lowers his aptitude to 90% and turns his body female (ch 199). Furious but pragmatic, she joins Fang Yuan to escape the mountain and travel the world (ch 199, 200).
* **First gen Gu Yue:** Rank 5 blood wight zombie. A former Middle Continent sect member who betrayed his martial brother for a blood sea inheritance (ch 192). He planned for centuries to farm his descendants' blood to upgrade his own aptitude (ch 194). In the second timeline, Fang Yuan throws him out of his own protective barrier (ch 197), and he is ultimately beheaded by Lord Sky Crane (ch 198).
* **Lord Sky Crane:** Rank 5 expert from the Middle Continent. Vengeful and bitter, he rides a myriad beast king crane (ch 192). He slaughters the clan leaders (ch 191) and eventually kills First gen Gu Yue (ch 198). He survives the mountain's freezing by sealing himself in a jade coffin (ch 199). Upon breaking out, he kidnaps Fang Zheng to use him as a blood-tracking compass to find Fang Yuan (ch 199).
* **Tie Xue Leng (Divine Investigator):** Already dead, but his final backup plan activates post-mortem, temporarily binding First gen Gu Yue and sealing his Gu (ch 193).
* **Gu Yue Fang Zheng:** Fang Yuan's twin. Survives the crane disaster using "Moon Raiment Gu" before taking a blow to the head and falling down a cliff (ch 199). Lord Sky Crane finds him frozen in the ice, rescues him, and abducts him to the Middle Continent (ch 199).
* **Gu Yue Bo:** Rank 4 Gu Yue Clan Leader. Fights bravely to protect Fang Yuan during the crane attack, assuming Fang Yuan has a ten extreme physique (ch 191). Killed by Lord Sky Crane (ch 191).
* **Gu Yue Mo Yan, Gu Yue Dong Tu (and his wife), Xiong Jiao Man:** Prominent clan members. All are brutally killed during the battles and ensuing massacres inside the village (ch 193, 194, 198).

## Gu and cultivation
* **Crane Enslavement Gu:** Rank 5. Allows the user to enslave a myriad beast king, thereby controlling an army of 10,000 beasts (ch 192). Used by Lord Sky Crane.
* **Kinship Bloodworm:** Rank 5. Crystal clear like red agate. Points toward blood relatives of the target (ch 192). Cruelly refined over decades by harvesting heart blood from close, living relatives (ch 192). 
* **Life-retaining Jade Burial Gu:** Rank 5. Suspends the user's life indefinitely and creates an extremely durable transparent jade coffin for defense (ch 192, 199). Used by Lord Sky Crane.
* **Raise Eyebrows & Exhale Gu:** Rank 5. Causes the user's eyebrows to grow tens of meters long, acting like strong steel roots to bind a target (ch 192). Connects the apertures of the user and victim, rapidly draining both of their primeval essence (ch 192). 
* **Demon Suppression Iron Chain Gu:** Tie family Gu. Creates phantom chains that solidify to bind hands and feet, while also sealing the target's aperture and heavily taxing their primeval essence (ch 193).
* **Problem Nipped in the Bud Gu:** Tie family Gu. A yellow talisman that extracts Gu worms from a target's aperture and seals them in light spheres on the ground (ch 193).
* **Blood Skull Gu:** Rank 4. Looks like a child's crystal skull filled with blood stripes (ch 193). It drinks the blood of the user's slain relatives and regurgitates a fragrant blood fountain (ch 194). This fountain soaks into the user's aperture, permanently raising their aptitude percentage (ch 194, 198).
* **Yin Yang Rotation Gu:** Rank 4 healing Gu. Appears as a black and white Taiji light sphere holding two chasing Gu (ch 194). The Yin and Yang Gu bring the dead back to life, but permanently lower their aptitude by 10% and reverse their sex (ch 199). Fang Yuan uses the Yin Gu to revive Bai Ning Bing, keeping the Yang Gu as leverage (ch 199).
* **Water Curtain Skyflower Gu:** Rank 4. Creates a spherical shield that blocks Rank 5 attacks, but prevents anyone (even the owner) from entering or exiting (ch 194). 
* **Blood Curtain Skyflower Gu:** Rank 5. Fused by First gen Gu Yue. Creates a red spherical shield (six mu in size). It blocks Rank 5 attacks and allows the owner to exit (but not re-enter) (ch 194). It cannot be moved or turned off, naturally dissipating after a set time (ch 194). Must be destroyed to be activated (ch 194).
* **Spring Autumn Cicada:** Rank 6. Allows the user to travel upstream in the river of time, returning to the past (ch 196). Extremely dangerous to use; requires the user to self-detonate their body and cultivation as fuel (ch 196). 
* **Cultivation mechanics:** 
  * Primeval essence naturally recovers in a normal light wall aperture (ch 192). 
  * Zombies have dead apertures and cannot naturally recover essence (ch 192).
  * Stone apertures (created by Stone Aperture Gu) also lose recovery ability (ch 196). However, the blood fountain from the Blood Skull Gu clashes with the stone aperture, reverting it to a natural light wall while destroying the temporary cultivation boost it provided (ch 198).
  * Aptitude brackets: 40-59% is C grade, 60-79% is B grade, and 80% and above is A grade (ch 198). The ten extreme physiques possess 100% aptitude (ch 199).

## Creatures and nature
* **Iron Beak Flying Cranes:** Wild beasts native to the Middle Continent (ch 191). They possess the strength of boars, rock-breaking claws, and iron beaks (ch 191). They travel in flocks of 10,000, led by hundred, thousand, and myriad beast kings (ch 191). 
* **Shuttle Swordfish:** Spindle-shaped black fish the size of a textile shuttle, with sharp heads acting like augers and bodies covered in grim light scales (ch 200). They are carnivorous predators that hunt in packs of hundreds or thousands in large rivers (ch 200). They shoot out of the water like arrows to pierce prey but will not swim in shallow water (ch 200).

## Events and conflicts
* **Crane disaster:** Lord Sky Crane attacks the three clans in the wilderness with 10,000 cranes (ch 191). With no fortifications, half the Gu Masters are wiped out instantly, and the three clan leaders are killed (ch 191). 
* **Rank 5 Battle:** Lord Sky Crane fights First gen Gu Yue above the Gu Yue village (ch 192). The village is devastated by the collateral damage. Tie Xue Leng's post-mortem trap binds First gen, draining his essence and stealing his Gu (ch 193).
* **The First Massacre:** First gen Gu Yue deploys the Blood Curtain Skyflower shield to trap his descendants, slaughtering them to upgrade his aptitude with the Blood Skull Gu (ch 194).
* **Time Reversal:** Cornered by two Rank 5s, Fang Yuan self-detonates to fuel the Spring Autumn Cicada, reversing time back to the middle of the Rank 5 battle (ch 196).
* **The Second Massacre & Betrayal:** In the new timeline, Fang Yuan throws First gen Gu Yue out of his own protective barrier, trapping him outside with Lord Sky Crane (ch 197). Inside the barrier, Fang Yuan and Bai Ning Bing butcher the remaining clansmen (ch 198). Fang Yuan uses the Blood Skull Gu to raise his aptitude to 90% (ch 198). Outside, Lord Sky Crane beheads First gen Gu Yue (ch 198).
* **The Great Freeze:** Bai Ning Bing unleashes his dying physique, causing a massive glacier that freezes Qing Mao Mountain and traps Lord Sky Crane (ch 199). Fang Yuan resurrects him (ch 199). 
* **River escape:** Fang Yuan and Bai Ning Bing flee on a makeshift Qing Mao bamboo raft down the Yellow Dragon River (ch 200). After five days of sailing, they are attacked by a swarm of Shuttle Swordfish that destroy the raft (ch 200). Fang Yuan uses Sky Canopy Gu to survive just long enough to reach a shallow beach (ch 200).

## Mood and time
* **Tone:** Apocalyptic, desperate, and utterly ruthless. The arc ends in an outright massacre of innocent clansmen for personal gain, followed by the complete ecological freezing of Qing Mao Mountain. 
* **Time:** The final battles happen over a few hours. The escape on the bamboo raft takes about five days of continuous sailing (ch 200).

## Recurring objects
* **Primeval stones:** Desperately chewed and swallowed whole by First gen Gu Yue to rapidly recover essence during battle (ch 194, 196). 
* **Bamboo raft:** Made hastily from cut Qing Mao bamboos tied with hemp ropes, featuring a simple mast and a shabby white sail (ch 200). 

## Open questions
* **Distance:** The exact distance from Qing Mao Mountain to Bai Gu Mountain is unstated, though it takes roughly five days of fast downriver travel on a raft to get near it (ch 200).
