# T-043c0b4b2a — bounded clock gate (S75)
2026-10-31 · D-266–D-267 · pure wrapper, NOT live-wired.

CastClockGate.new(freshBridge, policy) exclusively owns bridge event/tick/teardown calls. Policy: pendingCapacity, futureSeconds, maxWaitSeconds, maxSampleError, correctionPerSecond. These are caller-supplied testable constraints, NOT production tuning. Future tolerance plus bridge maximum lifetime must fit bridge tombstone TTL. The first finite server sample anchors the clock. Later time advances from monotonic local elapsed time, slewing toward valid nearby samples by at most elapsed * correctionPerSecond (0 <= rate < 1). Invalid/outlier samples increment badSamples but local time still drives expiry. Same-local-time packets cannot accumulate clock correction; backward/nonfinite local time refuses without changing state. Outliers do not trigger an implicit rebase that jumps phases.

accept key/timeline either forwards immediately or queues a small future acceptance. Pending storage is capped and immutable, duplicates neither replace first payload nor extend deadline. Every tick/event prunes/drains; local deadline wins over readiness after long idle gaps. cancel removes its key before advancing/draining, so a terminal arriving exactly when the future acceptance becomes due cannot flash a start. Unknown cancellations use the bridge tombstone; overflow additionally keeps one future-time retirement watermark to prevent resurrection after capacity frees (may conservatively suppress unrelated older cosmetics). No unbounded history, and no damage eligibility changes.

The owner MUST tick without messages. badSamples/expired/refusals are gate counters; bridge renderFailures/resourceRefusals/resources.cleanupFailures remain separate. Gate tick success means valid clock processing, NOT successful rendering: the fallback step must observe bridge failures, including delayed/drained ones. No essential warning fallback exists yet. Callers must not drive/destroy the underlying bridge directly after wrapping it. Gate/reducer/renderer/disposer calls are synchronous/non-yielding; reentrant gate mutations refuse. Destroy closes gate admission and removes pending references, then disposes bridge resources once.
