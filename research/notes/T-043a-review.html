Make the ice feel
shaped, not spawned.

A complete Rimeshard direction: deliberate hand poses, faceted ice, four unmistakable silhouettes — starting with the left-click combo, not another layer of particles.

Choose the language

A · Recommended

Faceted frost

Sharp hand-shaped splinters, brief mist, fractures building on targets. Preserves the ranged kit and keeps the enemy visible.

B · Alternative

Snowstorm caster

Flowing snow ribbons and swirling clouds. Atmospheric, but less distinctive and easier to obscure a phone screen.

C · Alternative

Ice gauntlet brawler

Arm armour and heavy punches. Physical, but would imply melee and demand combat redesign rather than visual refinement.

Borrow from Blox Fruits: clear poses, recognisable shapes and a start → peak → tail rhythm. Do not borrow: its assets, weapons, fruit identities or screen-filling whiteout.

One family. Four different signatures.

M1Needle / the everyday attack

Left flick → right counter → two-hand paired release. Give the body a purpose and the finisher a visible settling pose.

Hit: small directional chips. Miss: clean travel and fade. No default camera shake.

Keep 3-shot chain, 45-stud range and 1.2 s finisher recovery. Prove this slice with the owner first.

ZShard Fan / open the hand

A compact opening sweep launches five separate splinters. A short release ribbon connects the hand to the fan, without a fog wall.

Keep: narrow forward footprint and existing per-target hit cap.

First resolve the current 0.2 s telegraph vs immediate server launch mismatch. Art must not lie about timing.

XRime Fall / command the cold

Lift, mark, then drive four grouped falls downward. An exact boundary and low broken rim provide weight without hiding the enemy.

Keep: radius 7, 0.6 s warning, four damage ticks. Caster and observers share the same timeline.

Sparse air particles; one clean downward silhouette. Repeated ticks never stack camera shake.

CShatter / break what you built

Compress the frost, then fracture it at the targets. Three Chill stacks earn a distinct crown break; fewer stacks get a smaller crack.

Empty cast: only a hand release. No fake explosion, hit sound or camera punch.

Keep Chilled-only targeting. Stack-aware visuals require real server state, not guessed client puffs.

The quality gate

Approve the direction → build M1 → review it in motion → only then expand.

Every later move follows the accepted M1 grammar. “Tests pass” means the code works, not that the ability looks good. The parent task stays open until the owner is satisfied with the entire kit.

  • See the body and enemy through every effect. Differentiate moves without colour or labels.
  • Keep damage, range and cooldowns; reconcile timing explicitly under tests.
  • Budget hypothesis: 24 shard meshes, 96 live decorative particles, 8 trails/beams, 4 sound voices across the kit. Device evidence decides the final caps.
  • No measured device FPS or live visual approval yet. T-042b functional playtest remains open.
