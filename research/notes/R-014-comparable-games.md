## 2. Genre patterns
- Cultivation games on Roblox loop around: meditate for energy → break through realms → quests → combat. [15][16]
- Players like: the feeling of a breakthrough, idle/auto-farm while away, flashy high-level abilities. [15][16]
- Players dislike: bugs in test builds, slow grinds, difficulty spikes, exploits ("infinite qi"), too many systems too early. [17][18]
- Games fail when abandoned, when saves are lost, or when "infinite growth" leaves no endgame tension. [18]
- Permadeath gives tension (Deepwoken) but drives burnout; Type Soul's non-permanent progression keeps a steadier base. [8][9]

## 3. Gaps Robby can own
1. **Cultivation with real action combat.** Cultivation games are small (≤1.4k CCU) and mostly meditate/idle; anime RPGs have the combat. Nobody combines Rell Seas-style combat with a cultivation ladder at high quality.
2. **Living creatures as abilities.** Fruits/techniques are static. Taming, feeding, fusing and combo-ing creatures is a collection + crafting loop no listed game has (except the RI fan game, which is incremental, not action).
3. **An original, takedown-proof world.** The big anime RPGs and the RI fan game lean on others' IP. An original world is safe to grow and monetise.
4. **Endgame tension without permadeath.** Use rival clans, scarce resources and risky fusions instead of losing the character.
5. **Respect for time.** Short sessions that still progress; avoid "meditate for an hour".
