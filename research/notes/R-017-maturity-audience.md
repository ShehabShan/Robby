# R-017 — Content maturity & audience (S4, 2026-10-30)

Goal: pick a Roblox maturity label and decide how to adapt the source's dark tone. [n] marks a source.

## 1. Labels (Maturity & Compliance questionnaire)
| Label | Violence | Blood | Fear | Who can play |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimal | Occasional mild | Light, unrealistic | Occasional mild | Everyone [1][2] |
| Mild | Repeated mild | Heavy but unrealistic | Repeated mild | Broad, incl. young players [1][2][4] |
| Moderate | Non-graphic, realistic injury | Light realistic | Moderate (realistic wounds, disfigurement) | Older players [1][4][5] |
| Restricted | Strong, graphic, dismemberment | Heavy realistic | — | 18+ ID-verified only; hidden from under-18s [1][6][7] |

## 2. Rules
- Restricted experiences need an 18+ ID-verified creator and are invisible in search/recommendations for under-18s. [6][7][8]
- Banned at every label: torture, extreme gore, serious physical/psychological abuse, animal abuse, war crimes. [3][6]
- Moderate and strong violence must be declared in the questionnaire. [5][9]
- Discovery: R-001 found most Roblox players are young and the first minutes decide retention (see RESEARCH.md).

## 3. Recommendation (D-024)
- Target **Mild**: repeated fantasy combat with stylised, unrealistic hit effects keeps the widest audience.
- **Moderate** only if a key feature truly needs it. **Never Restricted** (cuts out most of Roblox).

## 4. Adapting the source's tone
| Source element | Robby version (Mild) |
|---|---|
| Ruthless, amoral protagonist | Player chooses clan loyalty or outcast cunning; no cruelty to innocents |
| Creatures devour each other in a jar | Creatures **merge** in a glowing crucible — no eating or corpses |
| Killing, blood, gore | Enemies are knocked out / dissolve into energy; coloured hit sparks, no blood |
| Poison and curses | Status effects shown as colours/icons, not suffering |
| Scarcity, betrayal, "cruel world" | Scarce resources, rival clans, risky fusions, territory fights |
| Dark philosophy | Short in-world sayings about ambition and choice |

## 5. Unknowns
- Exact age bands per label (Roblox changes them); real discovery difference Mild vs Moderate.
