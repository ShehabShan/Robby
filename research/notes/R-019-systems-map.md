## 2. First ability for the spike
- RI's iconic early attack: a rank 1 crescent creature, marked on the brow and moved to the palm; a cutting motion throws a pale-blue crescent blade at range; ~10% of a rank 1 pool per blade; eats moon-flower petals; later fuses into a rank 2 version. [1][2][3][4]
- Other popular early creatures: permanent-strength boars; an essence-refining worm. [5][6]
- **Pick: Crescent Kith (rank 1, Moon path) → move "Crescent Cut".** It is flashy, ranged, simple to validate, and has a natural rank 2 Crucible upgrade later.
- Build route: crescent from neon parts/Beam + Trail + particles, tweened out; spherecast/blockcast hitbox along the path. [7][8][9][10]
