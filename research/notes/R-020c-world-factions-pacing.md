# R-020c — RI world structure, factions & progression pacing (S7, 2026-10-30)

Deepens R-011 §1 (World & factions). Sources are fan wikis/summaries, not the novel (depth unverified).
IP rule (D-014): RI terms appear only here, to describe the source. Game content uses Robby mirror names
(Reaches, Veils, Clans, Rogues, Halls, Wild Surge, Legacy Vault, Warden, Talent, Rank).

## 1. Findings (sourced) → Robby mirror

| # | RI finding | Src | Robby mirror (original) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The world has five regions, each with a distinct society: sect-based rich centre, mountain/forest clans, cold-grassland tribes, a water region, a desert. | [1][2][3] | **Five Reaches** keep their own society, not just a biome: Mistvale = bloodline Clans; Ember Steppe = nomad bands; Tidefall = island houses; Stonecrown = merit Halls; the Hollow = no rule. Original names. |
| 2 | Regional walls separate regions; they suppress the strength of anyone crossing, and suppress stronger cultivators more. | [3][4][5] | **Veils** between Reaches: crossing needs a Rank gate *and* a short Veil trial where your stats are briefly damped. Stops high-Rank players farming starter zones (anti-grief). |
| 3 | Mountain clans are bloodline + seniority hierarchies: a leader, an elder circle, halls for missions, assets and evaluations. | [6][7][8] | **Clan hub:** an NPC Elder Council hands out missions and monthly stipends by contribution rank; a Mission Hall board = daily bounties. Player Clans copy the same ladder. |
| 4 | Heirs and factions inside a clan compete through business and combat, backed by rival elders. | [8][9] | **Elder favour:** two NPC elders with rival agendas; side quests raise one's favour and unlock that elder's Weave hints. Light politics without heavy text. |
| 5 | The centre uses talent-based sects (master–disciple, no bloodline), with higher entry standards and loyalty tests. | [10][11][12] | **Halls (Stonecrown):** join by passing an entrance trial (skill test, not Talent grade); Hall rank gives access to rare Kith and Crucible recipes. A second faction flavour mid-game. |
| 6 | "Righteous" = clan/sect security and resources; "demonic" = loners outside protection who take by force; both commit atrocities — a sociopolitical split, not good vs evil. | [13][14][15] | **Clans vs Rogues** (already GDD §9): keep it morally grey. Rogues get bounty-hunting, black-market Crucible recipes and no stipend; Clans get stipends, safe storage and guards. No "evil" label. |
| 7 | Villages face recurring beast tides: small packs escalate into a massed assault led by beast kings ranked by pack size (hundred / thousand / myriad). | [16][17][18][19] | **Wild Surge:** a server-wide event every ~3 real days. Waves escalate (Pack → Horde → Swarm) to a pack-lord boss; defenders earn Marks and Shards. Strong co-op hook for M2. |
| 8 | Inheritance grounds sit in pocket worlds left by dead masters; a spirit made from their last wish tests successors; entry needs keys; Gu use is restricted inside; trials are lethal. | [20][21][22][23] | **Legacy Vaults:** instanced dungeons opened with keys from bosses. A **Warden** spirit sets a theme rule (e.g. "no Flame moves") and a trial chain; rewards = unique Kith/Weaves. Great repeatable endgame content. |
| 9 | Each rank is a big jump: higher-rank essence is far more potent; breaking into the next rank needs a full breakthrough. | [24][25][26][27] | **Rank gap:** +1 Rank ≈ ×1.6 Qi potency (P-007 to tune). Opponents one Rank higher are shown with a warning colour; two higher = near-unwinnable. Makes each breakthrough feel huge. |
| 10 | Talent grades set pace: low talent reaches rank 1 senior in ~3 years; top talent reaches rank 4 in ~10. Rank 3 masters are full-fledged, often clan elders. Resources speed things up. | [28][29][30] | **Pacing:** Talent sets a soft cap and Qi regen, never a hard wall (resources + Mastery let anyone climb). Rank 3 = "full cultivator" social milestone (title, house slot). Fits GDD §7 hours. |

## 2. → Implications for Robby (GDD change candidates for P-006)
1. Give each Reach its own society + faction flavour (row 1, 5).
2. Veils with Rank gate + damping trial; anti-grief in starter Reaches (row 2).
3. Clan hub: Elder Council, Mission Hall, contribution stipends, rival-elder favour (rows 3, 4).
4. Keep Clans vs Rogues morally grey with distinct perks (row 6).
5. Wild Surge server event (M2) and Legacy Vault dungeons (M2–M3) (rows 7, 8).
6. Steep, visible Rank gap and Rank 3 milestone; Talent = soft cap only (rows 9, 10).

## 3. Unknowns
- Server load of a Wild Surge with 30+ players on mobile (needs a perf test in M2).
- Real story-time pacing is only summarised; Robby hours are design choices (GDD §7, P-007).
